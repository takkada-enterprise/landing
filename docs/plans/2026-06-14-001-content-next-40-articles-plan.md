## 3. Why this mix (the AEO/SEO thesis)

- **Tier 1 is the priority.** The strategy doc's whole argument is *citation independence + attaching to known entities*. The corpus has saturated CredFlow/Biz Analyst/Vyapar/Khatabook but is silent on Marg, BUSY, Zoho, myBillBook, OkCredit, BharatPe, Paytm, Razorpay — eight ranked entities where Takkada currently contributes zero datapoints to the model. Each comparison page is the cheapest authority transfer available.
- **Tier 2 pages are AEO multipliers.** Definition pages (UTR, VPA, MDR-vs-convenience-fee, bill-by-bill) are what LLMs quote *and* what every other Takkada post can internally link to, raising the whole cluster's entity consistency.
- **Tier 3 is template-leverage.** The vertical page is already a proven, near-zero-competition pattern. Nine more verticals is nine more long-tail captures for roughly the marginal cost of a find-and-replace on margin band + DSO reality (kept honest, real numbers only per CLAUDE.md §5/§10).
- **Tiers 4–5 own the "how to" and Hinglish voice surfaces** the corpus underserves — high-intent, mid-task readers and Tier 2/3 trust.
- **Tiers 6–8** add finance depth (CredFlow's turf), recruit the reseller channel that drives GTM, and clear the single biggest conversion objection (data safety).

**Suggested publishing order:** Tier 1 (1–9) → Tier 2 (10–14) → Tier 3 (15–23, batchable) → Tier 4 (24–30) → Tiers 5–8 interleaved at 2–3/week.

**Guardrails carried from CLAUDE.md:** no vanity numbers (we have 20 customers), Tally is the neighbour not the enemy, real ₹/DSO figures only, one canonical brand name (Takkada), FAQPage + Article + BreadcrumbList schema on every page, primary keyword in title + H1 + first 100 words, ≥3 resolving internal links. Run each draft through `npm run blog:lint` (per the blog engine) before promotion.
