## At the dealers visited, the eighteen ledgers

At the dealers visited lists three things at the shops that got a stamp: orders by anyone, payments received, and overdue outstanding at those dealers.

Gupta Stores is one of Raju's eighteen. Raju's 14 cartons sit in both columns, because they are his order at a dealer he visited. The owner also called Gupta at noon and booked a crate himself. That order is not Raju's. It still sits on At the dealers visited, because anyone's order at that ledger counts there.

A UPI credit lands on Gupta Stores at 3 PM. ₹8,400 against an old bill. That payment is on the dealer. It is never credited to Raju. Booked on these visits does not grow because money came in. Overdue outstanding sits on that column too. Gupta Stores still carries ₹1,20,000 past terms. The owner reads the visit next to the overdue, which is the conversation [salesman-wise collection accountability](/blog/salesman-wise-collection-accountability/) is for on the books, without putting the receipt on Raju's name.

The two columns exist because Station Road can look busy when the owner booked the crate, or look dry when Gupta paid an old bill and Raju still booked nothing. Payments stay on the ledger. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I see which salesman visits actually produced orders?**

A: Open Field Visits, then Outcomes. Booked on these visits lists orders attributed to that salesman. At the dealers visited lists orders by anyone, payments received, and overdue outstanding at those dealers. Filter by salesman or Everyone. Feed is the stamps. Outcomes is what sat around them.

**Q: What is visit to order conversion in field sales?**

A: Reading the two Outcomes columns together. Booked on these visits is his orders. At the dealers visited is everything at those ledgers, including other people's orders and payments that stay on the dealer. Conversion is those two counts against the visits that actually happened.

**Q: Are payments credited to the salesman who visited?**

A: No. Payments received at the dealers visited sit on the dealer. They are never credited to the salesman. A UPI credit on Gupta Stores at 3 PM does not add a row to Booked on these visits.

**Q: Does the salesman pick an outcome type after the visit?**

A: No. The visit carries an optional free-text note. He types what happened, or he leaves it blank. The order, if any, is the Sales Order in Tally. The note is not a coded result.

**Q: Can an order booked by the owner show on At the dealers visited?**

A: Yes. At the dealers visited includes orders by anyone at those dealers. If the owner booked a crate on Gupta Stores after Raju's stamp, that order sits on the dealer column. It does not sit on Booked on these visits for Raju.

**Q: Does Outcomes work when the visit is still on the phone?**

A: Visit check-in and check-out are saved on the phone without signal and upload when signal returns. Creating an order, invoice or receipt needs a working data connection. Outcomes reads visits and orders that have already landed. A stamp still labelled Saved on this phone is not on the owner's feed yet.
