Takkada and Counts both connect Tally to a phone and a browser, show your books on mobile, help you follow up on receivables and share invoices and ledgers on WhatsApp. Across the 16 distributor features in this comparison, Takkada offers 16 and Counts offers 4, counting a partial feature as half and based on each app's public pages. The biggest difference is direction. Counts is built around dashboards, GST tracking and receivables, read from Tally through its desktop app, and its public pages do not describe creating vouchers that post back into Tally. Takkada is built around entries made on the phone: invoices, orders, delivery challans and credit notes that land in Tally, a salesman module with geo-tagged visit check-ins, a dispatch loading sheet, AI voice calls to overdue parties, and a zero-MDR UPI link on the invoice. Several of those are Takkada add-ons, and the table marks which.

## What is Takkada?

Takkada is a mobile and web app for Indian distributors that run their books in TallyPrime or Tally ERP 9. It connects the whole distribution day to Tally: salesman order booking, sales invoices, delivery challans, dispatch loading, WhatsApp delivery of invoices, UPI payment links with 0% MDR, automatic payment reminders on WhatsApp and email, AI voice calls, and automatic matching of payments to invoices. Every entry made in Takkada posts back into Tally, and your Tally data files stay on your own computer. Some of these are plan features and some are add-ons; the table below says which.

## What is Counts?

Counts is a Tally companion app by Metabook Technologies for iOS, Android and web. It syncs Tally data through a desktop app and offers dashboards, GST tracking, receivables tools, one-click reminders and sharing of invoices and ledgers on WhatsApp.

## Invoices and reminders from your own WhatsApp number

With Takkada, invoices and payment reminders can go out from your own WhatsApp Business number, so the retailer sees your firm's name and replies to you, the same number he already saves and trusts. This is an add-on, and Meta bills the messages to you directly. We did not find sending from the customer's own number on Counts's public pages. Reminders also go out by email, and an AI voice call can follow up with parties who do not respond.

## Where Counts is ahead

Counts puts dashboards and GST tracking at the centre, which suits an owner or accountant who mainly wants to read the books and keep compliance in view. If nobody on your team will create entries from a phone, that focus may be all you need.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the difference between Takkada and Counts?**

A: Both apps put Tally data on mobile and the browser and help with receivables. Takkada also creates vouchers on the phone that post into Tally, and adds delivery challans, salesman check-ins, a dispatch loading sheet and UPI collection matched back into Tally.

**Q: Can I create Tally entries from the Counts app?**

A: Creating vouchers from mobile is not mentioned on Counts' public pages (checked November 2026). Takkada creates sales invoices, orders, delivery challans, and credit and debit notes on mobile and posts them into Tally.

**Q: Does Counts have a salesman app?**

A: A salesman or field-team module is not mentioned on Counts' public pages. In Takkada each salesman books orders for his own parties, and the Team Sales module on the Copilot plan records a photo, location and time at each customer visit.

**Q: Does Takkada work with TallyPrime and Tally ERP 9?**

A: Yes. Takkada works with TallyPrime and Tally ERP 9 through a connector on the Windows PC where Tally runs. Every invoice created in Tally appears in Takkada, and every entry made in Takkada posts back into Tally.

**Q: Which Tally mobile app can make AI calls to collect payments?**

A: Takkada places AI voice calls to customers about their dues as an add-on module billed per connected minute, alongside automatic reminders on WhatsApp and email.

**Q: Does my Tally data move to the cloud with Takkada?**

A: Takkada syncs with the Tally installed on your own computer. Your data files stay where they are today. Stop using Takkada and your Tally keeps working exactly as before.
