## How to Check Party Outstanding in Tally: What It Means

To check party outstanding in Tally is to ask one question: how much does this retailer still owe me right now, and against which bills. The "outstanding" is the sum of every invoice you have raised to that party minus every receipt you have logged against them. Tally keeps this running figure per ledger, and it can break it down bill by bill so you can see which specific invoice is unpaid and how old it is.

The number itself is easy. The timing is the hard part. A distributor needs the party outstanding at the counter, while the retailer is asking for fresh credit, not at 9 PM when the van is already back and the stock is already gone. That is the gap this guide closes. First the exact desktop steps to check a party's outstanding balance, then the faster way to pull the same partywise outstanding from a phone on the route. For the wider report behind this single number, the [partywise outstanding statement in Tally](/blog/partywise-outstanding-statement-tally/) walks the all-parties view.

## The Exact Steps to Check a Party's Outstanding Balance in Tally

Here is the desktop path, the one almost every Tally user reaches for. These steps are for Tally Prime on the office Windows machine where your company data lives.

1. From the **Gateway of Tally**, click **Display More Reports**
2. Open **Statements of Accounts**
3. Choose **Outstandings**
4. Select **Ledger** (this gives you one party at a time; **Group** gives you all sundry debtors together)
5. Type or pick the party's ledger name from the list
6. Tally now shows that party's outstanding, broken down bill by bill, with the date, the pending amount, and the age of each bill

If you want every party in one screen instead of one at a time, at step 4 choose **Group**, then select **Sundry Debtors**. That gives you the full partywise outstanding for your whole retail network, which you can sort by amount or by age. The bill-level detail behind each party is the [bill-by-bill against reference](/blog/bill-by-bill-against-reference-tally/) mechanism Tally uses to tie a receipt to the exact invoice it clears.

A note on accuracy: this report is only as current as your last entry. If the accountant has not yet keyed in this morning's receipt, the outstanding on screen is stale by exactly that one payment. For a distributor checking before extending credit, a few hours of lag can be the difference between a safe sale and a bad debt.

## How to Read the Bill-by-Bill Outstanding View

Once the party's outstanding is on screen, the columns tell a specific story. Reading them right is what turns a number into a decision.

| Column | What it tells you | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Date | When the bill was raised | The clock on the credit period starts here |
| Ref No. | The invoice or bill number | The reference you quote in a reminder |
| Pending Amount | What is still unpaid on that bill | The collectable figure, not the original bill |
| Due on | The date the bill should clear | Anything past this is overdue |
| Overdue by days | Age of the unpaid bill | Tells you which bill to chase first |

The figure that matters most for a credit decision is not the total at the bottom. It is the spread of the ageing. A party with ₹1,75,000 outstanding sitting entirely inside the agreed 30-day window is healthy. The same ₹1,75,000 with ₹90,000 of it aged past 60 days is a warning. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the exact path to check party outstanding in Tally?**

A: In Tally Prime, go to Gateway of Tally, then Display More Reports, then Statements of Accounts, then Outstandings, then Ledger, and select the party. Tally shows that party's outstanding balance broken down bill by bill. Choose Group instead of Ledger, then Sundry Debtors, if you want every party's outstanding in one screen.

**Q: Can I check a party's outstanding in Tally on my mobile?**

A: Tally Prime has no native phone app, so you check party outstanding on mobile through a companion layer that mirrors your Tally data to the phone. It installs a connector on the office machine and shows the same partywise outstanding and bill-by-bill ageing on your phone, usable from the field while the office PC stays where it is.

**Q: Why is the outstanding on my phone different from Tally on desktop?**

A: If the two differ, it is almost always a sync timing gap. The desktop figure updates the instant the accountant keys an entry; the mobile figure updates when the connector next syncs. A good mobile layer syncs continuously, so the gap is seconds, not hours. If a receipt was logged on the phone, it should also post back into Tally so both sides match.

**Q: How do I see which bill is overdue, not just the total?**

A: The bill-by-bill outstanding view in Tally lists each unpaid bill with its date, reference number, pending amount, due date, and how many days it is overdue. Read the overdue-by-days column to know which bill to chase first. The total at the bottom hides the ageing; the bill detail is where the risk shows.
