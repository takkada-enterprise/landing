Takkada and Livekeeping overlap more than most Tally mobile apps. Both put TallyPrime or Tally ERP 9 on a phone and in a browser, handle unlimited companies, create vouchers that post into Tally, generate e-invoices and e-way bills, share invoices on WhatsApp and record field check-ins. Across the 16 distributor features in this comparison, Takkada offers 16 and Livekeeping offers 8, counting a partial feature as half and based on each app's public listings. Takkada goes further across the distributor's day. It adds delivery challans, a customer ordering link, a dispatch loading sheet, AI voice calls to overdue parties, a zero-MDR UPI link on the invoice and payments matched back into Tally, and it can cancel an e-invoice or e-way bill from the phone. Livekeeping sells e-invoice and e-way bill as a paid add-on tier, while on Takkada they come with the Assurance plan.

## What is Takkada?

Takkada is a mobile and web app for Indian distributors that run their books in TallyPrime or Tally ERP 9. It connects the whole distribution day to Tally: salesman order booking, sales invoices, delivery challans, dispatch loading, WhatsApp delivery of invoices, UPI payment links with 0% MDR, automatic payment reminders on WhatsApp and email, AI voice calls, and automatic matching of payments to invoices. Every entry made in Takkada posts back into Tally, and your Tally data files stay on your own computer. Some of these are plan features and some are add-ons; the table below says which.

## What is Livekeeping?

Livekeeping is a Tally-on-mobile app for Android, iOS and web. It offers 20+ business reports, voucher creation from mobile with a maker-checker step before entries reach the desktop, sharing of invoices and ledgers on WhatsApp, WhatsApp reminders for outstanding bills, GPS-enabled sales tracking with punch in/out, e-invoice and e-way bill, and unlimited companies.

## Invoices and reminders from your own WhatsApp number

With Takkada, invoices and payment reminders can go out from your own WhatsApp Business number, so the retailer sees your firm's name and replies to you, the same number he already saves and trusts. This is an add-on, and Meta bills the messages to you directly. We did not find sending from the customer's own number on Livekeeping's public pages. Reminders also go out by email, and an AI voice call can follow up with parties who do not respond.

## Where Livekeeping is ahead

Livekeeping's maker-checker step lets an owner approve each mobile entry before it reaches Tally, and it ships updates often, so its mobile GST flow is well worn. If mobile invoicing with GST documents is all you need, it covers that.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the difference between Takkada and Livekeeping?**

A: Both apps put Tally on mobile and the browser, create vouchers, generate e-invoices and e-way bills, and record field check-ins. Takkada also offers delivery challans, a customer ordering link, a dispatch loading sheet and AI voice calls, plus UPI collection matched back into Tally.

**Q: Does Livekeeping have dispatch and proof of delivery?**

A: We did not find dispatch, loading sheets or proof of delivery on Livekeeping's public listings (checked November 2026). Takkada's Dispatch Load on the Copilot plan builds the van load and prints the loading sheet, and the Proof of Delivery add-on gives drivers a login with a geo-tagged photo at each drop, with cash or UPI approved by the office.

**Q: Does Livekeeping collect payments by UPI?**

A: UPI payment links and automatic payment matching are not mentioned on Livekeeping's public listings. With the Payment Collection add-on, Takkada puts a UPI link on the invoice at 0% MDR and matches each payment to its invoice.

**Q: Does Takkada work with TallyPrime and Tally ERP 9?**

A: Yes. Takkada works with TallyPrime and Tally ERP 9 through a connector on the Windows PC where Tally runs. Every invoice created in Tally appears in Takkada, and every entry made in Takkada posts back into Tally.

**Q: Which Tally mobile app can make AI calls to collect payments?**

A: Takkada places AI voice calls to customers about their dues as an add-on module billed per connected minute, alongside automatic reminders on WhatsApp and email.

**Q: Does my Tally data move to the cloud with Takkada?**

A: Takkada syncs with the Tally installed on your own computer. Your data files stay where they are today. Stop using Takkada and your Tally keeps working exactly as before.
