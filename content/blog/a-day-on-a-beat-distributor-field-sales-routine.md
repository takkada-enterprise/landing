## What does a distributor salesman's day look like with a beat app?

A distributor field sales daily routine on this book is Station Road on Tuesday. Raju has twenty-four kirana shops on that stretch. Manoj has GS Road. The other three have Fancy Bazaar, NH-37 and Beltola. The [Salesman app for Tally](/salesman-app-tally) is the day from the first shop to the last rupee. [Beat-wise sales management](/blog/beat-wise-sales-management-tally-distributors/) is the named lists. [What a beat is in distribution sales](/blog/what-is-a-beat-in-distribution-sales/) is a name plus a dealer list, with no date, weekday, sequence or visit target. Raju decides which shutter he photographs first. Today's load does not order the walk.

## Nine in the morning, he opens his beat

Nine o'clock. Raju unlocks the phone in the parking lane off Station Road. Dealers sit in beats, the fixed stretches this market already runs on, with a salesman on each. He opens Today's load for Station Road. Pending orders from the last three days including today. Saturday's leftover carton sits behind an older pending chip, because Saturday is outside Tuesday's window.

[Create beats and assign a salesman](/blog/create-beats-and-assign-salesman-mobile/) already put Gupta Stores on Station Road and Raju on that list. He still chooses the first door. Today's load does not tell him to stand at Gupta Stores at 10:15.

In the godown, around the same hour, Dispatch is open: what goes out today. Pending orders across Station Road, GS Road, Fancy Bazaar, NH-37 and Beltola total into one item list, then a Load sheet per beat with the per-dealer breakdown. [The dispatch load sheet](/blog/dispatch-load-sheet-for-distributors/) is that totalling. Raju sees Station Road on the phone. The dispatcher sees all five sheets.

Beats also work on the web. The owner can have set Station Road at the desktop on Monday. Raju cannot check in from a browser. Visit capture waits for the street.

He WhatsApps the godown at 9:10. "Station Road ka load nikal, Gupta pehle." What they print is the 3-day window as of this morning, not a distinct next-day pile.

## He checks in, and the visit proves itself

10:40. Gupta Stores. One tap marks the visit with the time and the place, and it carries a geo-tagged, time-stamped photo. The camera opens. Gallery is not an option. He photographs the shutter. Global Positioning System (GPS) location with accuracy, device time, his name, Gupta Stores, optional note. [What actually proves he went](/blog/salesman-visit-tracking-photo-proof/) is those facts on one record.

If the phone reports a mock or fake location, check-in is refused. No visit is recorded. The refused attempt still shows to the owner. Field Visits is available on request. The owner's feed defaults to today.

Check-out later records duration, At the dealer 12 minutes, and distance from where the visit started, Checked out 40 m from where the visit started. [How long he was at the dealer](/blog/visit-duration-and-check-out-field-sales/) is that second stamp. Duration shows as unreliable when the check-out leg fails its own trust check.

Visit check-in and check-out are saved on the phone without signal and upload when signal returns. Creating an order, invoice or receipt needs a working data connection. Station Road drops behind concrete between two kiranas. The stamp can wait. The voucher cannot.

Visit capture is phone-only. Manoj on GS Road is doing the same loop on his own list. Five people, five handsets, one book.

## The order is booked against live stock

After check-out, still at Gupta Stores, Raju builds the order. Live stock sits next to every item, so nobody promises what the godown does not have. Fourteen cartons, ₹42,000. He saves it. It lands in Tally as a Sales Order the moment it saves, with the pending quantity tracked until it ships.

[Salesman order taking without re-entry](/blog/salesman-order-to-tally-without-reentry/) is that landing. He can raise an order after check-out or at any other time. Order creation is not gated on Team Sales. What [field teams actually need from a Tally app in India](/blog/salesman-app-tally-india/) is still the party in front of him and the voucher in the books before he walks to the next shutter.

A [field order collection app for Tally](/blog/field-order-collection-app-tally/) is this counter, this stock column, this save. The office does not retype Tuesday from a WhatsApp list at 9 PM.

If signal is dead behind the shutter, Take an order waits. He moves to the main road. The visit is already Saved on this phone. The Sales Order needs data.

## The godown loads the van from one sheet

The orders booked on a route total themselves into one load sheet. So many bags, so many crates, then the same list dealer by dealer. Station Road's sheet has Gupta Stores' lines, including Tuesday's ₹42,000 once it is in the 3-day window, and Monday's leftover that was already pending at nine.

As the van empties, deliveries get ticked off against each shop. Mark delivered, Mark all delivered, Undo if the wrong shutter was ticked. At 2:40 the delivery boy ticks Gupta Stores. [Mark orders delivered without touching Tally](/blog/mark-orders-delivered-without-touching-tally/) is that tick. Delivered is a Takkada-only fact. Tally is not told an order was delivered. The Sales Order stays open until the office converts it to an invoice the way they already do.

Dispatch and the Load sheet also work on the web. The godown can run them at the desktop. The tick still does not write a voucher. Fancy Bazaar, NH-37 and Beltola each have their own sheet. Five vans, five lists, one totalling.

## The invoice on WhatsApp, then night on Team Sales

When the invoice is raised, it posts with items, GST and the Tally number series. The PDF and a Pay now link reach the retailer on WhatsApp. Gupta Stores can pay before Raju has walked three shops down, and the UPI receipt matches itself against that bill, at 0% MDR on UPI collections, no transaction cap, no monthly fee.

Tuesday's ₹42,000 is still a Sales Order until the office bills it, or until Raju raises the invoice from the phone on a visit that has signal. Either way the PDF is the Tally invoice.

By night the owner already knows how the day went. Visits and orders per salesman as they land. He opens Team Sales at 8 PM on the web. Period is Month. Basis is Invoices. Raju's August target is ₹8,00,000. Orders sit at ₹9,20,000. Invoices sit at ₹6,40,000. Gupta Stores paid ₹8,400 at 3 PM against an old bill. That receipt sits on the dealer. It does not climb Raju a row. [A sales team leaderboard from Tally](/blog/sales-team-leaderboard-for-distributors/) is that stack. [Salesman targets and commission from Tally](/blog/salesman-targets-and-commission-from-tally/) is the figure under it. Commission is worked from orders or invoices. There is no collections ranking.

He used to call five people. "Kitna hua aaj?" The evening call asking everyone what they did stops being necessary. Station Road stays a named list. There is no scheduler, no map, and no all-day tracking. A distributor field sales daily routine on this book ends on the same Tally sales the office already trusts.
