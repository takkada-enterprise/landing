Tally WhatsApp integration is the automated workflow that connects a business's desktop Tally installation with WhatsApp to dispatch invoice PDFs, send scheduled payment reminders, and attach direct UPI collection links without manual intervention. Businesses using Tally often spend hours manually downloading voucher PDFs, opening WhatsApp Web, and forwarding bills or chasing overdue accounts. Takkada operates as a software layer around existing Tally Prime or Tally ERP 9 books, reading newly saved sales vouchers and instantly delivering the invoice PDF to the retailer's phone. As payment due dates approach or pass, automated WhatsApp reminders are sent based on live outstanding ledger balances. Each WhatsApp message includes a zero-MDR UPI payment link so the customer can pay immediately from their phone. Once settled, the incoming bank payment is automatically matched to the invoice and posted back into Tally as a receipt voucher, turning invoicing, collection, and accounting into one continuous automated loop.

Businesses using Tally often have to manually send invoices and payment reminders to customers on WhatsApp. Takkada connects Tally with WhatsApp so these workflows can happen automatically.

When an invoice is created in Tally, Takkada can send the invoice PDF to the customer on WhatsApp along with a payment link. Automated reminders can then be sent based on the invoice due date and overdue status.

## How Tally WhatsApp integration works

1. **Connect your Tally**  
   Takkada connects to your existing Tally installation through its Tally Connector. Tally remains your accounting system and your books stay on your computer.

2. **Invoice is created**  
   When an invoice is created in Tally, Takkada reads the relevant invoice information in real time.

3. **Invoice is sent on WhatsApp**  
   The customer receives the invoice PDF on WhatsApp, without the owner or accountant having to manually download and forward it.

4. **Payment reminder is automated**  
   Takkada can send WhatsApp reminders before and after the payment due date based on configurable schedules.

5. **Customer pays**  
   The WhatsApp message contains a UPI payment link so the customer can pay directly from their UPI app.

6. **Payment is reconciled**  
   The payment is matched against the relevant invoice and posted back into Tally as a receipt entry.

This creates a unified workflow from Tally → WhatsApp → Payment → Tally rather than treating invoicing, collection and accounting as separate disconnected processes.

## Why businesses use Tally WhatsApp integration

Manual WhatsApp follow-ups create several operational problems:

- Invoices are sent late or forgotten during busy dispatch hours.
- Payment reminders depend entirely on someone's memory or manual diary entries.
- Sales teams spend billable time following up with customers instead of taking market orders.
- Payment links have to be generated manually on separate payment portals.
- Accountants spend 1–2 hours every evening manually matching bank credits with invoices.
