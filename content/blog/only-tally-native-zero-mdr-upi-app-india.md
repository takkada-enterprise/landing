## What a Real Zero MDR UPI App Must Prove

A zero MDR UPI app should mean exactly what it says: you receive the full amount a retailer pays, with nothing deducted. The trouble is that "zero MDR" has become a marketing phrase, and several apps use it while still costing you money in a different line. So a real zero MDR UPI app has to prove three things, not one.

First, no percentage cut. Second, no per-transaction fee, in any name. Third, for a distributor on Tally, the receipt has to land back in Tally cleanly without retyping. Most apps clear one or two of these. ## The "0% MDR" That Still Charges ₹3 a Transaction

This is the most common way a zero MDR UPI app is not actually free. The app advertises "0% MDR" prominently, then charges a flat fee on every transaction, for example ₹3 a receipt. Because ₹3 is a rupee figure and not a percentage, the "0% MDR" claim survives while your cost does not.

The damage depends on your volume. At 200 receipts a day, ₹3 each is ₹600 a day and around ₹1,80,000 a year. On a ₹400 receipt, ₹3 is effectively a 0.75% charge. So the first thing to check in any zero MDR UPI app is not the MDR; it is whether there is any per-transaction fee at all. ## Why Tally-Native Is the Harder Half

Most collection apps are standalone. They take a payment and leave you to record it in your accounts yourself. For a distributor running Tally, that means someone still types each receipt into Tally at night, which is the work a mobile collection tool was supposed to remove.

Tally-native means the opposite: when a retailer pays, the receipt posts back into Tally as a proper receipt voucher, against the right invoice, with no retyping. This is genuinely harder to build than a payment screen, which is why a zero MDR UPI app that is also Tally-native is rare. ## The Reconciliation Test Most Apps Fail

Here is the test that separates a real distributor tool from a payment screen. Three retailers each pay ₹14,320 on the same day and send near-identical WhatsApp screenshots. Can the app tell which payment cleared which invoice?

A standalone app cannot; it just shows three payments of the same amount. A Tally-native app that matches on the UTR, the unique reference each UPI payment carries, tied to the specific invoice, resolves all three correctly and posts each to the right party. This is the difference between a clean ledger and an hour of nightly guessing, as the explainer on [tally payment reconciliation on mobile](/blog/tally-payment-reconciliation-on-mobile/) describes. A zero MDR UPI app that fails this test is only solving half the problem.

## How to Verify the Claim Yourself

Do not take "zero MDR" on faith. Ask any zero MDR UPI app four direct questions:

1. Is there any per-transaction fee, in any name, on any plan
2. Is there a monthly or platform fee
3. Does a receipt post back into Tally automatically as a voucher
4. How does it reconcile two payments of the same amount
