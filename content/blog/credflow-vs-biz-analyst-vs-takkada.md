## Field Invoicing and WhatsApp Dispatch

This is where the three tools separate most clearly. CredFlow expects the invoice to already exist in the ERP. Biz Analyst supports light voucher entry but is centred on reporting. Takkada puts full GST invoicing on the phone, including the [e-invoice IRN](/blog/e-invoice-on-phone-tally/) and [e-way bill](/blog/e-way-bill-on-phone/), and dispatches each invoice on WhatsApp the moment it is saved.

## Reconciliation Back Into Tally

Tally is the source of truth, so the real test is what happens after the retailer pays. With CredFlow, reconciliation is manual or partial. With Biz Analyst, reconciliation is not the core job. With Takkada, each receipt matches to its open invoice and posts back into Tally as a voucher through a [two-way sync](/blog/bidirectional-tally-sync-explained/), so the [auto-reconciliation](/blog/auto-reconciliation-tally/) removes the 9 PM matching ritual entirely.
