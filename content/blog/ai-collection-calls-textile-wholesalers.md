AI calls for textile wholesalers keep the long credit cycle moving between the agent's visits. In Takkada, you open Send reminders now, tick the retailers and boutiques who are past their 90 or 120 days, and tap Call with AI. An AI agent calls each one in the language you set, names your firm and the amount due from your Tally books, and asks when they will pay. The answer comes back as a follow-up on that party: Will pay with an amount and date, No answer, Wrong number, Disputes amount or Refused, with a note and, once ready, the recording. If a retailer disputes a bill over a damaged than or a rate difference, the note records the reason and the invoice. If he misses a promised date, the AI can call again on its own a set number of days later. For a wholesaler whose money is spread across a hundred towns, every overdue account hears from you before it turns into a year-old balance.

## The textile credit cycle

A textile wholesaler in Surat, Bhiwandi, Erode or Kolkata might have 150 to 300 retailers across several states. Goods go out before the season, on 90 to 120 day credit, sometimes longer in practice. A single retailer can owe ₹3,50,000 across bills from Diwali and the wedding season.

Collection runs through agents. The agent visits each town once or twice a season, and the retailer pays when the agent sits across from him. Between visits, nothing happens. A balance that should have closed in 120 days drifts to 200, and by then the retailer is buying from someone else on fresh credit.

## Calls between the agent's visits

Send reminders now lists every party that owes money, with the overdue total, the number of overdue bills and the age of the oldest bill, from the same figures as your Bills Receivable screen. You can search by name or filter by Tally ledger group. If your ledgers are grouped by agent or by state, one tap narrows the list to the parties one agent handles.

Tap Call with AI, and the confirm sheet shows the number of parties and the cost before you tap Call now. A party the AI cannot call is named on the result sheet with the reason, such as no phone number on the ledger or the monthly AI call limit reached.

## Disputes surface early

Long credit cycles hide disputes. A retailer holds back ₹40,000 because one lot came with colour variation, and nobody hears about it until the agent visits four months later. On an AI call, that comes out as Disputes amount, with the reason and the invoice in the note. With the Recovery dashboard on, it sits on the party's follow-up timeline, and the agent can settle it on his next visit with the facts already in hand.

A retailer who says "season ke baad, 10 tarikh" gets a promise saved with the amount and date. If the date passes without payment and "Call again when a promise is missed" is on, the AI calls again after the delay you set, tagged AI · auto.

Choose the one most of your retailers speak. Tone is Polite or Firm, and a callback number lets the retailer ring your office or your agent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can the AI call retailers in different states in different languages?**

A: The language is one setting for your business. All AI calls go out in the language you pick from the twelve offered.

**Q: Can I call only one agent's parties?**

A: Yes, if your Tally ledgers are grouped by agent. Filter by that ledger group on Send reminders now and tick those parties.

**Q: Can I play back what the retailer said?**

A: Yes. Each AI call row on the party's timeline has a Play recording button once the recording is ready.

**Q: Does an AI call change the retailer's balance in Tally?**

A: No. The call result is a follow-up in Takkada. The balance changes only when a payment is entered in Tally.
