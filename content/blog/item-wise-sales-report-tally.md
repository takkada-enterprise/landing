## What an Item-Wise Sales Report Shows

A distribution business is really a bet on which products the market wants. The item-wise sales report is where that bet gets scored. It tells the owner, for any period, how much of each SKU actually sold, in cases and in rupees, and that is the number his entire purchasing rhythm depends on.

The pattern is familiar in every godown. A small band of SKUs moves constantly, a wider band moves steadily, and a tail sits gathering dust while quietly holding working capital hostage. The item-wise report makes the three bands visible, and it makes the shifts visible: a line that used to move and has gone quiet, a new SKU that is picking up faster than expected. Both are worth acting on before the next purchase order goes out.

## Where Product Sales Live in Tally

In Tally Prime, every product is a stock item, and inventory reports gather how each one moved. Stock Summary shows opening, inward, outward, and closing quantity and value for each item over the period. For a pure sales cut, the item-wise or Movement Analysis reports under Display, then Inventory Books, isolate outward movement, which is your selling by product.

Set the period to this month or a custom range and each item shows quantity sold and sales value. Sort by value or quantity and the fast movers rise to the top. Drill into a single item and you see which parties bought it and in what lots. It is the inventory counterpart to the [party-wise sales report](/blog/party-wise-sales-report-tally/): one tells you who bought, this one tells you what they bought.

## The Purchasing Decisions It Drives

This is the report that touches cash most directly, because every reorder is money leaving the business.

| The report shows | The purchasing move |
|---|---|
| A top SKU selling faster than usual | Reorder early so you do not go out of stock mid-month |
| A steady line dipping quietly | Check the market, hold the next order rather than overstock |
| An SKU that has not moved in weeks | Run a scheme or a return before capital sits dead in it |
| A new item climbing quickly | Widen the order and push it to more parties |

Get these right and the godown carries the stock that sells and less of the stock that does not, which is the difference between healthy working capital and money trapped on shelves.

## Item Sales, Stock in Hand, and Reorder

Item sales only tell you half of the reorder question. The other half is what is still on the shelf. Selling fifty cases of a line means nothing for reordering until you also know whether eighty cases are sitting in the godown or five.

The two read together are the reorder signal: high sales plus low stock in hand equals reorder now; low sales plus high stock equals hold and clear. That pairing is why the item-wise sales report and the [stock summary report](/blog/stock-summary-report-tally-mobile/) belong on the same screen, so the owner judges movement and inventory in one look instead of two.
