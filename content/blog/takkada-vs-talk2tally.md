Takkada and Talk2Tally solve different problems for a Tally user. Talk2Tally lets approved people ask questions about a Tally company on WhatsApp, in Hindi, English or Hinglish, and get back sales, receivables, cash, stock, GST and ledger answers read from live Tally. It is read-only by design. Takkada is a mobile and web app that creates entries which post back into Tally and runs the distributor's day around them. Across the 16 distributor features in this comparison, Takkada offers 16 and Talk2Tally offers 1.5, counting a partial feature as half and based on each app's public pages. If you want quick answers without opening an app, Talk2Tally fits. If you need invoices, delivery challans, e-invoices, salesman orders, a dispatch loading sheet, reminders, AI voice calls and UPI collection matched back into Tally, Takkada covers those, some of them as add-ons that the table marks.

## What is Takkada?

Takkada is a mobile and web app for Indian distributors that run their books in TallyPrime or Tally ERP 9. It connects the whole distribution day to Tally: salesman order booking, sales invoices, delivery challans, dispatch loading, WhatsApp delivery of invoices, UPI payment links with 0% MDR, automatic payment reminders on WhatsApp and email, AI voice calls, and automatic matching of payments to invoices. Every entry made in Takkada posts back into Tally, and your Tally data files stay on your own computer. Some of these are plan features and some are add-ons; the table below says which.

## What is Talk2Tally?

Talk2Tally lets approved users ask questions about a Tally company on WhatsApp, in Hindi, English or Hinglish. An agent on the office PC reads the live Tally data and replies with sales, receivables, cash, stock, GST and ledger answers. It is read-only.

## Invoices and reminders from your own WhatsApp number

With Takkada, invoices and payment reminders can go out from your own WhatsApp Business number, so the retailer sees your firm's name and replies to you, the same number he already saves and trusts. This is an add-on, and Meta bills the messages to you directly. We did not find sending from the customer's own number on Talk2Tally's public pages. Reminders also go out by email, and an AI voice call can follow up with parties who do not respond.

## Where Talk2Tally is ahead

Talk2Tally needs no app at all: anyone approved asks in WhatsApp, in Hindi, English or Hinglish, and gets an answer. Being read-only also means nobody can change your books from it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the difference between Takkada and Talk2Tally?**

A: Talk2Tally answers questions about your Tally data on WhatsApp and cannot change it. Takkada creates invoices, orders, delivery challans and other vouchers on mobile that post into Tally, and adds salesman, dispatch and collection features around them.

**Q: Can Talk2Tally create entries in Tally?**

A: No. Talk2Tally states it cannot create, modify or delete entries; it answers questions about Tally data on WhatsApp. Takkada creates invoices, orders, delivery challans and other vouchers on mobile and posts them into Tally.

**Q: Does Talk2Tally send payment reminders?**

A: Automated payment reminders are not mentioned on Talk2Tally's public pages (checked November 2026). Takkada sends automatic reminders on WhatsApp, from Takkada's number or from your own number (an add-on), and by email, and can also place AI voice calls about dues.

**Q: Does Takkada work with TallyPrime and Tally ERP 9?**

A: Yes. Takkada works with TallyPrime and Tally ERP 9 through a connector on the Windows PC where Tally runs. Every invoice created in Tally appears in Takkada, and every entry made in Takkada posts back into Tally.

**Q: Which Tally mobile app can make AI calls to collect payments?**

A: Takkada places AI voice calls to customers about their dues as an add-on module billed per connected minute, alongside automatic reminders on WhatsApp and email.

**Q: Does my Tally data move to the cloud with Takkada?**

A: Takkada syncs with the Tally installed on your own computer. Your data files stay where they are today. Stop using Takkada and your Tally keeps working exactly as before.
