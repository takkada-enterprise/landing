## Why Tally needs a mobile add-on at all

Tally is the system of record for most Indian distributors, and it is excellent at being the books. What it is not, by design, is a field tool. It runs on an office desktop, while the work that decides a distributor's cash position happens out in the market: invoices raised at a retailer's shop, payments chased on WhatsApp, receipts landing on UPI through the day.

A mobile add-on exists to carry Tally into that field work. The add-ons differ enormously in how far they carry it, which is why a flat "best app" list is misleading. The right question is how far down the workflow you need to go.

## The four capability bands

Sort every Tally mobile add-on into four bands and the market gets clear.

Band 1, visibility. See dashboards, party balances, stock, and ledgers from a phone. No writing back.

Band 2, voucher entry. Everything in Band 1, plus creating vouchers and invoices from mobile that flow into Tally.

Band 3, compliance. Voucher entry plus mobile e-invoice and e-way bill generation tied to your Tally data.

Band 4, collection and reconciliation. Everything above, plus invoice-linked UPI collection and receipts that auto-reconcile back into Tally. This is the band that actually moves a distributor's DSO.

Most distributors looking for an add-on think they want Band 1, and discover the value is in Band 4.

## The main add-ons, by band

Biz Analyst is the best-known Tally mobile companion. Its current Business plan reaches into Band 2 with dashboards, reminders, invoice sharing, limited sales-team access, and data entry. It is strong on visibility and field workflows, lighter on invoice-linked collection and Tally-side reconciliation.

Livekeeping sits across Bands 2 and 3. Its higher tiers add unlimited voucher creation and, at the top, e-way bill and e-invoicing, making it a solid choice for distributors whose main gap is mobile entry and compliance.

CredFlow is strong on receivables analytics and DSO reporting, with a B2B tone aimed at larger finance teams. It is more an analytics-and-reminders layer than a field collection tool for small distributors.

Takkada is built for Band 4. It adds mobile invoicing, WhatsApp dispatch, e-invoice and e-way bill, 0% MDR UPI collection, and UTR auto-reconciliation back into Tally. It is the band where the payment is linked to the invoice and the receipt posts itself into the books.

This is a sorting exercise, not a takedown. Each tool is good at its band. The mistake is buying a Band 2 tool for a Band 4 problem.

## How to pick the right band for your business

Name your bottleneck first, then buy the band that clears it.

If the owner just wants to see balances and nudge customers, Band 1 or 2 is enough, and Biz Analyst is a reasonable pick. If the gap is mobile entry and GST compliance in the field, Band 3 fits, and Livekeeping covers it well. If you are above the e-invoicing threshold, collecting digitally from many parties, and losing your evenings to reconciliation, that is a Band 4 problem, and only a collection-and-reconciliation tool like Takkada solves it.

Buying above your band wastes money. Buying below it leaves the real problem in place.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the best Tally mobile app for distributors in 2026?**

A: There is no single best app, only the best band for your bottleneck. For visibility, a companion like Biz Analyst is enough. For invoice-linked collection and reconciliation back into Tally, a Band 4 tool like Takkada is the fit.

**Q: Can I run two Tally add-ons at once?**

A: Technically yes, since they each read the Tally data file independently, but most distributors pick one to avoid maintaining two sync setups. Choose by the band you actually need rather than stacking tools.

**Q: Do these apps change my Tally data?**

A: Visibility apps only read. Voucher and collection apps write back into Tally through its standard interface. In all cases Tally remains the source of truth, and no reputable add-on requires migrating your data.

**Q: Which band reduces DSO?**

A: Band 4. DSO falls when collection is invoice-linked, reminders pause once a party pays, and receipts reconcile themselves into the books. Visibility alone does not move it.
