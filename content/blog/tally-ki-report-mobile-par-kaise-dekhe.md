## Do groups: Financial Statements aur Action Reports

Financial Statements: Trial Balance, Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, MIS Reports. Trial Balance, P&L aur Balance Sheet synced Tally books se seedha padhte hain. Trial Balance flat list hai. Line se ledger mein drill nahi. Group se sub-group se ledger Balance Sheet ka kaam hai. Jab synced books foot nahi karti, Balance Sheet par Difference row aati hai. [Trial Balance phone par](/blog/trial-balance-tally-mobile/) yahi list hai.

MIS Reports 12-month grid hai, total column ke saath. Cell se voucher ya ledger mein drill nahi. MIS Reports+ ka hissa hai. Reports+ Copilot plan mein bundled paid add-on hai. Uske bina bhi Trial Balance, P&L, Balance Sheet khulte hain.

Action Reports: Collections, Bills Receivable, Bills Payable, Sales Analytics, Customer Analytics, Cross-sell Gaps, Inventory, Purchase Analytics, Daybook. Collections ke do tabs: Outstanding aur Payment behaviour. Payment behaviour, Sales Analytics, Cross-sell Gaps, Purchase Analytics aur Inventory ka Velocity tab Reports+ se khulte hain.

Daybook mein Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Custom, aur voucher-type chip. Baaki action reports mein zyadatar date filter nahi. Ageing aaj ke hisaab se hai. Ledger statement aur godown reports mein This FY, This Quarter, This Month, Last Month, Last FY, Custom.

Godown ka stock alag screen hai, Reports list ka filter nahi. Location wise quantity [godown ka stock mobile se](/blog/godown-ka-stock-mobile-se-kaise-dekhe/) mein hai. Salesman filter kisi report par nahi hai.

Shaam, godown band, Sales Analytics agar Reports+ on hai: kaun sa item hila, kaun si party ne uthaya. Customer Analytics: kaun kitni baar, kitne ka. Customer Analytics sirf PDF. Cross-sell Gaps woh items jo party ko uthane chahiye aur nahi utha rahi.

## Report WhatsApp par kaise share karein

Har exported PDF ya Excel phone ki share sheet se jaati hai. Wahan WhatsApp, email, Drive. Yeh honest path hai report ko chat mein daalne ka. Alag se har report par koi magic WhatsApp button nahi.

9:40 par Sharma bole "bhai, statement bhej do" toh woh report PDF nahi. Party page, bills page ya ageing report se ledger statement ya pending-bills statement. Send from my WhatsApp aapka khud ka WhatsApp kholta hai, message pehle se typed, PDF share sheet se. Takkada business number par app queued dikhata hai. Detail [report PDF WhatsApp par](/blog/share-tally-report-pdf-on-whatsapp/) aur [pending bills versus full ledger](/blog/pending-bills-statement-vs-ledger-statement-whatsapp/) mein hai.

Web app par manual WhatsApp send sirf message text hai, clipboard par copy bhi. PDF attachment phone-only hai. Chrome se file chat mein nahi baithti. Phone se export karo.

Stock aur godown par export nahi. Jorhat mein 30 carton hain ya nahi, yeh godown view hai, WhatsApp PDF nahi.

## Excel, PDF, CSV: kaun si file kis report ki

PDF aur Excel chooser in reports par hai: Trial Balance, Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Bills Receivable ageing, Bills Payable ageing, MIS, Payments, Purchase Analytics, Sales Analytics, Cross-sell Gaps, Inventory Velocity. Poori list [Excel export mobile se](/blog/export-tally-report-to-excel-from-mobile/) mein hai.

Receivables Ageing aur Customer Analytics sirf PDF, Excel nahi. Daybook PDF ya CSV. CSV ko Excel mat kaho. Tally-format export, jo Tally khud print karti hai, paanch statutory reports par: Trial Balance, Balance Sheet, P&L, Bills Payable, Bills Receivable. P&L aur Balance Sheet is format mein sirf current period ke liye.

CA ne "TB Excel mein bhej do" likha toh Trial Balance, chooser, Excel, share sheet. "Tally ka print" maanga toh Tally-format. Sharma ke statement ke liye Excel nahi, party document.

Payments report par Bill-wise ya FIFO toggle hai. Jo basis screen par khula hai, wahi Excel mein jaata hai. Bills Receivable ageing ki slabs phone par set ki hain toh sheet unhi buckets ko follow karti hai. File mein extra column nahi aata jo screen par nahi tha. Salesman-wise cut Excel se nahi niklega kyunki salesman filter report par hai hi nahi.

## Team mein reports kaun dekh sakta hai

Reports tab ki access team member par permission se lagti hai. Jis member ke paas permission nahi, usko reports dikhti hi nahi. Salesman party khol sakta hai, statement bhej sakta hai, lekin Trial Balance uske phone par tab tak nahi jab tak Reports permission na ho. [Kaun kaun si reports dekh sakta hai](/blog/who-can-see-which-reports-tally-team/) yahi all-or-nothing rule hai.

Owner Fancy Bazaar par khud kholta hai kyunki accountant office mein nahi, aur CA wait nahi karega. Permission salesman ko tab do jab usko reports dekhni hain. Default yeh mat samjho ki party access ke saath Reports bhi aa gayi.

Ek typical Tuesday ka phone use aise dikhta hai. 8:30 Outstanding, Sharma ₹1,85,000. 12:10 Daybook Today, saat sales voucher. 9:10 agle mahine CA ke liye Trial Balance Excel, share sheet, WhatsApp. 9:40 Sharma ke liye Pending Bills, party page se. Chaar alag files, chaar alag sawaal. Reports screen un sabko ek jagah rakhti hai. Har file phir bhi usi report se nikalni padti hai jiska sawaal poocha gaya.

Barpeta wale bhai ko MIS grid bhejna ho, Reports+ on ho, 12-month column August mein patla dikhe, toh grid ke cell se invoice nahi khulega. Daybook ya Sales Analytics alag se kholna padega. Jo log cell se bill expect karte hain unko pehle bol do, warna 4 baje extra WhatsApp aayega, "is number ka bill kahan hai".
