## How can my dealers place orders on their own without an app?

They open a web link. The owner sends it. The kirana does not install anything and does not make an account. He sees the catalogue, filters it with category chips, puts in quantities, and sends. The line lands in the merchant's inbox. One tap converts it.

That is the loop a customer order link on WhatsApp is for. Tuesday, 8:40. Gupta Stores used to send a voice note: "14 carton atta bhej dena, kal subah." Somebody in the office replayed it, guessed the SKU, and typed a Sales Order after tea. The link takes that typing off the morning. The kirana enters the 14. The owner converts.

The [Salesman app for Tally](/salesman-app-tally) is still how Raju books at the counter. [Salesman order taking without re-entry](/blog/salesman-order-to-tally-without-reentry/) is that counter job. The self-order link is the other door: the dealer who already knows what he wants, and would rather tap than wait for Raju's scooter. Both doors write a Sales Order. The [field order collection app](/blog/field-order-collection-app-tally/) still matters when Raju is standing in the shop. The link matters when he is not.

[Beat-wise sales management](/blog/beat-wise-sales-management-tally-distributors/) is the named lists this order will sit on once it is a Sales Order. Gupta Stores is on Station Road. After conversion, Station Road's pending pile can pick it up.

## Public link and personal link

There are two shapes of the same page. A public web link is the catalogue at a URL anyone with the address can open. A personal web link is the same idea, pointed at one dealer. Gupta Stores gets the personal one. A kirana the owner has not placed yet can be sent the public one.

Both are web links. The buyer places the order with no app and no login. The owner pastes a URL into WhatsApp, which is already how this market talks.

The difference the owner actually uses is who the link is for. Personal is Gupta Stores. Public is the URL he puts in the broadcast when a new Fancy Bazaar shutter asks "rate list bhej do." He still converts every order himself. Nothing becomes a Sales Order until that tap.

Personal links still ask the buyer for a mobile number. Gupta Stores' kirana types the number he uses on WhatsApp, then sends. The number rides with the order into the inbox. That is the identification step on a personal link. There is no password to forget and ring the office about.

## Customer order link on WhatsApp, from the owner to Gupta Stores

A customer order link on WhatsApp, on this book, is a paste. Tuesday, 8:42. The owner is still on GS Road traffic. Gupta Stores has already pinged. He opens the personal link for that party, copies it, and drops it in the thread: "Ispe order laga do, quantity khud daalna."

The kirana opens it in the browser already on the phone. Category chips sit across the top. He taps Atta, sets 14, and checks the line. Prices on that page are priced server-side. He types his mobile number and sends.

By 8:47 the order is in the inbox. Raju will see the line later on Today's load if it sits in the 3-day window. The morning voice-note pile is one dealer shorter.

Five salesmen, about 120 dealers, five roads. Station Road, GS Road, Fancy Bazaar, NH-37, Beltola. The owner sends the personal link to the shops that already order this way, and keeps Raju on the shutters that still want a visit. [What a beat is in distribution sales](/blog/what-is-a-beat-in-distribution-sales/) is still Station Road as a dealer list. The link does not replace the beat. It lets one shop on that list order before Raju arrives.

[Field teams on a Tally app in India](/blog/salesman-app-tally-india/) still need the counter. The customer order link on WhatsApp is for the dealers who will use a URL while Raju is elsewhere.

## Priced server-side, with category chips

Priced server-side means the rate the kirana sees is worked out on the merchant's side, from the books. He picks a quantity against a rate the office already holds. The kirana does not set the rate.

Category chips filter the catalogue so Gupta Stores finds atta without walking the full item list. Atta, oil, biscuits, or whatever this company's groups already are. A chip is a filter. The same items Raju would pick on the phone sit behind those chips.

The buyer needs a working data connection to open the page and send. Creating an order needs signal. Personal links still ask for the mobile number at the end of that send.

## The inbox and one-tap conversion

The order lands in the merchant's inbox. The owner opens it, sees Gupta Stores' 14 cartons, and converts with one tap. Until then the line is a request sitting with him.

One-tap conversion turns it into a Sales Order, the ordinary Tally voucher [Sales order on mobile](/sales-order-on-mobile) already describes for field booking. Same party. Same items. Same books. Nobody in the office retypes the 14. The kirana entered the quantity on the link, instead of Raju at the shutter.

The owner can leave it sitting. Conversion is his. A personal link does not write Tally on send. Public does not either. The inbox is the hold. Tuesday at 8:47 he converts Gupta Stores because the godown will load atta anyway. A Fancy Bazaar broadcast order at 9:10 he reads twice, because he does not know the shutter yet.

About 120 shops, five men, one inbox. The owner is the person who taps. Member permissions can later limit who sees which parties, the same discipline as [restricting salesman access](/blog/restrict-salesman-access-tally/). The tap itself is still conversion to a Sales Order.

## After conversion: Today's load and the Load sheet

Once the line is a Sales Order, it is a pending order until the office invoices it. That is the pile [pending orders not yet invoiced](/blog/pending-orders-not-yet-invoiced-distributor/) lists in one place. It is also the pile Today's load and Dispatch read.

At 9:15 Raju opens Today's load for Station Road: pending orders from a 3-day window including today. Gupta Stores' 14 cartons are on it if they still sit inside that window. The salesman did not take the order. He still has to know it is going out.

In the godown, Dispatch totals pending orders across Station Road, GS Road, Fancy Bazaar, NH-37 and Beltola into one item list, then a [dispatch load sheet](/blog/dispatch-load-sheet-for-distributors/) per beat with the per-dealer breakdown. Gupta Stores is on the Station Road sheet with those 14. The van loads from the totals. The delivery boys work from the sheet.

Older pending sits behind a chip on Today's load. A Friday link order that was never invoiced is still a Sales Order, just outside the 3-day window Raju sees first. Tally holds a Sales Order.
