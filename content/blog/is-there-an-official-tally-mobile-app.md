## The Short Answer on an Official Tally Mobile App

No, there is no official Tally mobile app. Tally Solutions, the company behind Tally Prime, does not publish an Android or iOS app that runs Tally on your phone. Anything you find on the Play Store or App Store with "Tally" in the name is a third-party companion app, not the official product on mobile.

This trips up a lot of distributors, who assume such a central piece of Indian business software must have an app and that they simply have not found it. They have not found it because it does not exist. Accepting that early saves a week of searching and points you at the question that matters: which companion layer fits your business. The full picture of what a phone can do with Tally is in the overview of [running Tally on mobile](/blog/tally-on-mobile/).

## Why Tally Solutions Never Built One

Tally Prime, and Tally.ERP 9 before it, is a Windows desktop product by design, going back to the late 1990s. The company data file lives on a single machine, and the multi-user model assumes desktop workstations on an office LAN. That architecture predates the smartphone era and was never re-platformed for mobile.

Rather than ship its own app, Tally built openness in. The Tally XML gateway lets other programs read from and write to Tally in a structured way. That is the deliberate choice: instead of one official mobile app, Tally allows an ecosystem of companion apps to connect. So the absence of an official Tally mobile app is not neglect. It is the reason a healthy set of companion apps exists at all.

## What People Mistake for an Official App

A few things get confused for an official Tally mobile app:

- **The Tally.NET remote feature**: a real Tally feature, but it is remote sign-in to the desktop for auditors, not a mobile app
- **A companion app with "Tally" in its name**: third-party, however official the branding looks
- **A cloud-hosted Tally desktop**: still the Windows product, just running on a remote machine you reach through a remote desktop client
- **Tally's own utilities or add-ons**: desktop tools, not a phone client

None of these is a native Tally app for your phone. Each is either a desktop feature or a third-party layer.

## What Every Real Mobile Path Actually Is

Strip away the confusion and there are four honest paths, all of them bridges to the office machine:

| Path | What it is | Native Tally app? |
|---|---|---|
| Remote desktop (AnyDesk, TeamViewer) | Screen-mirror the office PC | No |
| Tally.NET remote feature | Built-in remote sign-in for auditors | No |
| Read-only companion app | Syncs and shows your Tally data | No, third-party |
| Read + write companion app | Syncs both ways, lets you act | No, third-party |

Every row is a way to access Tally from a phone, and not one of them is an official Tally mobile app. The menu of these options for the Indian market is laid out in the guide to the [Tally mobile app in India](/blog/tally-mobile-app-india/), and the view-only tier specifically is covered in the breakdown of the [Tally Prime mobile app](/blog/tally-prime-mobile-app/) landscape.

## How to Choose a Companion App Well

Since the choice is always a companion app, judge it the way you would judge any partner that touches your books:

- **Read or read-and-write**: do you only need to view numbers, or also invoice and collect from the phone
- **Tally fidelity**: does a voucher created on the phone post as a clean, correct Tally voucher
- **Sync reliability**: how fast and how dependable is the connection through the office connector
- **Data handling**: where in India the data sits, who can read it, and how it is deleted when you leave
- **Field readiness**: does it work on cheap Android phones and hold up in 2G zones

Because Tally is the neighbour your whole business already runs on, the right companion app works with it cleanly rather than fighting it. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is there an official Tally mobile app from Tally Solutions?**

A: No. Tally Solutions does not publish a native mobile app that runs Tally on a phone. Every Tally app you see on an app store is a third-party companion app that connects to your Windows Tally and syncs data to the phone.

**Q: Why doesn't Tally have an official mobile app?**

A: Tally Prime is a Windows desktop product whose architecture predates smartphones, with the data file on one machine. Instead of building its own app, Tally exposes the Tally XML gateway so third-party companion apps can connect. The ecosystem of apps is the intended substitute for an official one.

**Q: Is the Tally.NET remote feature the official Tally mobile app?**

A: No. The Tally.NET remote feature lets someone sign into a Tally installation from elsewhere, mainly for auditors and CAs. It is a desktop remote-access feature, not a mobile app, and the interface on a phone is still the slow desktop screen.

**Q: Are third-party Tally companion apps safe to use?**

A: They can be, but you must vet them, because they read and sometimes write your real Tally data. Ask any vendor where in India the data is stored, who on their side can access it, and how it is deleted when you stop. A reputable companion app answers all three clearly.

**Q: If there is no official app, what is the best way to use Tally on mobile?**

A: For viewing only, a read-only companion app or remote desktop is enough. For invoicing and collecting from the field, a read-and-write companion app is the only path that holds up. Match the app to whether you need to look at numbers or act on them.

**Q: Will Tally Solutions release an official mobile app later?**

A: There is no announced official Tally mobile app, and we do not speculate on roadmaps. What exists today, and what distributors rely on, is the companion-app ecosystem built on the Tally XML gateway. Plan around what ships now, not what might.
