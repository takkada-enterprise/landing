Accounts receivable automation is the software-driven process of tracking outstanding customer balances, sending automated payment reminders, collecting payments digitally, and reconciling bank receipts directly with Tally without manual data entry. For businesses managing hundreds of open invoices across multiple retail buyers, tracking overdue accounts manually leads to delayed payment follow-ups, unclear ledger statements, and hours spent on manual bank reconciliation. Takkada automates this receivables cycle by synchronizing directly with existing Tally Prime or Tally ERP 9 books. It presents real-time outstanding ledger reports on mobile devices, dispatches scheduled WhatsApp payment reminders based on invoice due dates, and embeds zero-MDR UPI payment links so customers can settle dues directly from their phones. Once a payment lands in the distributor's bank account, Takkada matches the transaction to the corresponding open invoice and automatically posts a receipt entry into Tally, eliminating manual collection work while keeping financial records up to date.

Accounts receivable becomes difficult when businesses have hundreds of invoices, multiple customers and different payment terms.

Takkada automates the collection workflow around Tally—from identifying outstanding invoices to reminding customers, collecting payments and reconciling receipts.

## What is accounts receivable automation?

Accounts receivable automation is the use of software to reduce the manual work involved in collecting money owed by customers.

Instead of an owner or accountant manually checking outstanding invoices, calling customers, sending WhatsApp messages and matching bank payments, software can automate these steps.

## How Takkada automates accounts receivable

### Outstanding invoices
Takkada reads receivables from Tally in real time and makes customer-level outstanding information accessible on your phone.

### Automated reminders
Payment reminders can be scheduled before and after the invoice due date and sent automatically through WhatsApp. See how [Tally WhatsApp integration](/blog/tally-whatsapp-integration/) automates invoice and reminder dispatches.

Payments are matched against the relevant invoices and posted back into Tally without manual accounting entries. Learn more about [auto-reconciliation in Tally](/auto-reconciliation-tally/).

The result is a connected receivables workflow rather than separate, manual collection and accounting processes.

## Why automate accounts receivable?

Manual receivables management can lead to several business bottlenecks:

- Delayed payment follow-ups due to manual memory dependency.
- Missed reminders for overdue customer accounts.
- Unclear outstanding balances across sales and accounting teams.
- Time-consuming payment matching at the end of each business day.
- Delayed updates to Tally accounting records.

Automation makes receivables management systematic and predictable.

For distributors, this is particularly important because payment collection happens across many retailers while Tally remains the central system of record.
