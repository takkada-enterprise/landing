Takkada and TallyDekho both put TallyPrime or Tally ERP 9 on a phone, show sales, purchase, stock and ledger reports, create invoices and vouchers that sync into Tally, and set payment reminders. Across the 16 distributor features in this comparison, Takkada offers 16 and TallyDekho offers 3.5, counting a partial feature as half and based on each app's public pages. TallyDekho suits an owner who bills and follows up himself, and its Call Predict feature shows a party's transactions the moment they call. Takkada is built for a business with a team: a browser version, delivery challans, e-invoice and e-way bill from the phone, a customer ordering link, salesmen who see only their own parties, geo-tagged visit check-ins, a dispatch loading sheet, AI voice calls to overdue parties, and a zero-MDR UPI link on the invoice matched back into Tally. Several of those are add-ons, and the table marks which.

## What is Takkada?

Takkada is a mobile and web app for Indian distributors that run their books in TallyPrime or Tally ERP 9. It connects the whole distribution day to Tally: salesman order booking, sales invoices, delivery challans, dispatch loading, WhatsApp delivery of invoices, UPI payment links with 0% MDR, automatic payment reminders on WhatsApp and email, AI voice calls, and automatic matching of payments to invoices. Every entry made in Takkada posts back into Tally, and your Tally data files stay on your own computer. Some of these are plan features and some are add-ons; the table below says which.

## What is TallyDekho?

TallyDekho is a Tally-on-mobile app for Android and iOS with a Windows desktop connector. It shows sales, purchase, outstanding, ledger and stock reports, lets users create invoices, quotations and vouchers that sync with Tally, sets payment reminders while creating an invoice, backs up data to the cloud, and has Call Predict, which shows a party's transactions when they call.

## Invoices and reminders from your own WhatsApp number

With Takkada, invoices and payment reminders can go out from your own WhatsApp Business number, so the retailer sees your firm's name and replies to you, the same number he already saves and trusts. This is an add-on, and Meta bills the messages to you directly. We did not find sending from the customer's own number on TallyDekho's public pages. Reminders also go out by email, and an AI voice call can follow up with parties who do not respond.

## Where TallyDekho is ahead

Call Predict, which shows a party's transactions as soon as they call, is a feature Takkada does not have. TallyDekho also backs up your data to the cloud.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the difference between Takkada and TallyDekho?**

A: Both apps show Tally reports, create invoices that sync into Tally and set payment reminders. Takkada also offers browser access, delivery challans, e-invoice and e-way bill from the phone, a salesman module, a dispatch loading sheet and UPI collection matched back into Tally.

**Q: Does TallyDekho have a salesman app?**

A: A salesman or field-team module is not mentioned on TallyDekho's public website (checked November 2026). In Takkada each salesman books orders for his own parties, and the Team Sales module on the Copilot plan records a photo, location and time at each customer visit.

**Q: Does TallyDekho send invoices on WhatsApp automatically?**

A: TallyDekho says GST invoices can be shared from mobile but does not name the channel or describe automatic sending. In Takkada, every invoice can reach the customer on WhatsApp as it posts, from Takkada's number or from your own (an add-on), and with Payment Collection it carries a UPI link.

**Q: Does Takkada work with TallyPrime and Tally ERP 9?**

A: Yes. Takkada works with TallyPrime and Tally ERP 9 through a connector on the Windows PC where Tally runs. Every invoice created in Tally appears in Takkada, and every entry made in Takkada posts back into Tally.

**Q: Which Tally mobile app can make AI calls to collect payments?**

A: Takkada places AI voice calls to customers about their dues as an add-on module billed per connected minute, alongside automatic reminders on WhatsApp and email.

**Q: Does my Tally data move to the cloud with Takkada?**

A: Takkada syncs with the Tally installed on your own computer. Your data files stay where they are today. Stop using Takkada and your Tally keeps working exactly as before.
