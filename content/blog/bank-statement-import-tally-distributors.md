Importing a bank statement into Tally for distributors means taking the CSV, XLS or XLSX file your bank already gives you and turning each line into a receipt, payment or contra entry that you check before it posts. In Takkada, you open Bank & Cash, pick the bank account and tap Import statement, then choose the file. Each line shows a suggested ledger, and you confirm it, change it, allocate it to bills, split a loan EMI into principal and interest, or mark it not applicable. Lines that look already recorded are flagged with the date and amount of the matching entry, so a cheque you entered by hand last week can be spotted before it posts a second time. When you are ready, Post resolved lines creates the entries, and any lines you have not finished stay saved for later. The posted entries are the same receipts and payments you would make in the app, and they go to Tally the same way.

## What the bank account looks like at month end

A typical distributor sees 30 to 60 credits a week from retailers paying by UPI, NEFT and cheque, plus supplier payments, bank charges, a loan EMI and transfers to the cash account. In Tally, each one should be a receipt or payment against the right party, and ideally against the right bills.

The narration rarely helps. "UPI/CR/4321/SHREE GANESH" might be Shree Ganesh Traders or Shree Ganesh Kirana, and the accountant ends up matching credits against the ledger at nine at night before the month closes.

## From the bank file to entries

In **Bank & Cash**, open the bank account and tap **Import statement**. Choose the file, up to 10 MB, and pick the date format if your bank writes dates month first. If the statement is password protected, enter the password your bank uses; the app uses it once to open the file and does not save it.

Select the bank ledger in Tally that this statement belongs to. If the account number in the file looks like a different account, the app warns you before you go further.

## Resolving each line

The **Resolve Bank Statement** screen lists every line with what to do next:

- **Confirm** the suggested ledger, or **Change ledger** to pick another party or account.
- **Allocate to bills**, so a retailer's credit settles the right invoices.
- **Split EMI** for a loan instalment, entering principal and interest separately with an interest ledger.
- **Not applicable** for lines that should not be posted.
- Change the type when a line is a transfer between your own bank and cash accounts, or edit the narration.

A line marked **Possible duplicate** shows "Looks already recorded on" the date and amount of the existing entry. You can skip it, or post it anyway when it really is a second payment.

When some lines are ready, the button reads Post and the number of resolved lines. The rest stay saved to finish later, and an import summary shows what was posted and what is still open.

## What it does not do

The statement must be the bank's CSV, XLS or XLSX file. A photo or PDF of a statement is not read; the app asks for the spreadsheet file instead. The detailed walkthrough is on the [Bank statement import](/bank-statement-import-tally) page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Which statement file formats are supported?**

A: CSV, XLS and XLSX files up to 10 MB. Password-protected statements open with the password your bank uses.

**Q: Will it post a payment I already entered by hand?**

A: A line that matches an existing entry is marked Possible duplicate with that entry's date and amount. It posts only if you choose Post anyway.

**Q: Can one credit settle several invoices?**

A: Yes. Use Allocate to bills on the line and pick the invoices it pays.

**Q: Do I have to finish the whole statement in one sitting?**

A: No. Post the lines that are ready, and the rest stay saved to finish later.
