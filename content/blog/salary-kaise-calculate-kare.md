Salary kaise calculate kare, iska seedha formula yeh hai: monthly salary ko utne din se bhaag do jitne din ka aap mahina ginte ho, aur jo din bina chhutti ke absent the, unka paisa kaat do. Mahina teen tarah se gina jaata hai: 26 din (Sunday chhod kar), 30 din fix, ya calendar ke din, jo 28 se 31 hote hain. ₹15,000 ki salary par ek din ka paisa 26 din par ₹576.92, 30 din par ₹500 aur 31 din ke mahine mein ₹483.87 banta hai. Toh 31 din ke mahine mein ek din absent hone par salary ₹14,516.13 aati hai. Half day par aadhe din ka paisa katta hai, aur approved paid leave ka paisa nahi katta. Daily wage staff ki salary paid din guna din ka rate hoti hai, jaise 7 din × ₹550 = ₹3,850. Piece rate mein jitne pieces bane, guna rate per piece. Overtime upar se judta hai, aur advance ki kist neeche se katti hai.

## Key Highlights

- Ek din ki salary = monthly salary ÷ mahine ke din (26, 30 ya calendar). ₹15,000 par 31 din ke mahine mein ek din ₹483.87 ka hai
- Daily wage = paid din × din ka rate. Paid din matlab kaam ke din plus approved paid leave
- Piece rate = pieces × rate per piece. Overtime, bonus, advance aur katautiyan sab type ke staff par alag se lagti hain

## Mahina ginne ke teen tarike

Pehla faisla yeh hai ki aapka mahina kitne din ka hai. Jo bhi tarika chuno, saare staff ke liye aur har mahine wahi rakho, aur appointment letter mein likh do. Neeche ₹15,000 monthly salary par teeno tarike:

| Tarika | Kaise ginte hain | Ek din ka paisa (₹15,000 par) |
|---|---|---|
| 26 din | Mahine ke din minus 4 Sunday, har mahine 26 maana jaata hai | 15,000 ÷ 26 = ₹576.92 |
| 30 din | Har mahina 30 din ka, chahe February ho ya 31 din wala | 15,000 ÷ 30 = ₹500 |
| Calendar month | Jitne din us mahine mein hain: 28, 29, 30 ya 31 | 15,000 ÷ 31 = ₹483.87 (31 din ka mahina) |

26 din wale tarike mein ek din ka paisa sabse zyada hota hai, toh absent ka kata bhi zyada hota hai. Calendar month mein February mein ek din ka paisa zyada (15,000 ÷ 28 = ₹535.71) aur 31 din ke mahine mein kam hota hai.

## Absent, half day aur paid leave ka hisaab

Ek example lo. Sunil ki salary ₹15,000 hai, mahina October hai jisme 31 din hain, aur aap calendar month se ginte ho.

| Haalat | Hisaab | Salary |
|---|---|---|
| Poora mahina aaya | 15,000 | ₹15,000 |
| 1 din absent | 15,000 − 1 × 15,000 ÷ 31 | ₹14,516.13 |
| 1 din absent, 1 half day | 15,000 − 1.5 × 15,000 ÷ 31 | ₹14,274.19 |
| 2 din approved paid leave | Paid leave ka paisa nahi katta | ₹15,000 |

Agar wahi Sunil 26 din wale tarike par ho aur 2 din absent rahe, toh 15,000 − 2 × 576.92 = ₹13,846.15 banta hai. Isliye do log ek hi salary par alag-alag rakam le sakte hain, agar aapne unke liye alag tarike rakhe hain. Half day ka matlab hai aadhe din ka paisa katna, yaani 31 din ke mahine mein ₹241.94.

Weekly off aur company ki chhutti (jaise Diwali) monthly staff ke liye salary mein shaamil rehti hai, unka paisa nahi katta. Sirf woh din katte hain jo kaam ke din the aur banda bina approved leave ke nahi aaya.

## Daily wage staff ki salary kaise calculate kare

Daily wage staff ka formula ulta chalta hai. Yahan kaata nahi jaata, joda jaata hai: paid din × din ka rate. Paid din matlab jitne din kaam kiya, plus jo paid leave aapne approve ki.

Example: Anil packer hai, rate ₹550 din ka. Is mahine usne 24 din kaam kiya aur 1 din ki paid leave approve hui. Salary = 25 × 550 = ₹13,750. Mahine ke beech mein, jab sirf 7 din hue hon, toh 7 × 550 = ₹3,850.

Sunday aur chhutti ke din ka paisa daily wage wale ko milega ya nahi, yeh aapke state ke niyam aur aapke aur worker ke beech ki baat par depend karta hai, toh ek baar apne CA se poochh lo. Agar aap us din ka paisa dete ho, toh us vyakti ko monthly pay par rakhna aasaan rehta hai. Aur detail ke liye [daily wage salary app](/daily-wage-salary-app/) wala page dekho.

## Piece rate salary kaise nikale

Piece rate mein din nahi, kaam gina jaata hai. Har kaam ka rate tay karo, jaise bracket polishing ₹18 per dozen, aur roz ke pieces likho.

| Kaam | Mahine ke pieces | Rate | Rakam |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bracket polishing | 420 dozen | ₹18 per dozen | ₹7,560 |
| Packing | 900 box | ₹2.50 per box | ₹2,250 |
| Kul | | | ₹9,810 |

Pieces roz likhoge toh mahine ke end mein jhagda nahi hota.

## Overtime aur advance

Overtime ke liye pehle ghante ka rate nikalo: monthly salary ÷ din ÷ roz ke ghante. ₹15,600 salary, 26 din aur 8 ghante par ek ghante ka rate 15,600 ÷ 26 ÷ 8 = ₹75 hai. 6 ghante overtime normal rate par ₹450 aur double rate par ₹900 banta hai. Overtime kis rate par dena hai, yeh aapki policy aur aapke state ke niyam se tay hota hai, CA se confirm karo.

Advance ulta chalta hai. Agar Sunil ne ₹3,000 advance liya aur aapne ₹1,000 har mahine kaatna tay kiya, toh teen mahine salary se ₹1,000 kategi. Salary ka final hisaab: kamayi (din ki salary + overtime + bonus) minus katautiyan (PF, ESI, professional tax, advance ki kist, late fine).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Salary 26 din par calculate hoti hai ya 30 din par?**

A: Dono chalte hain, aur calendar month bhi. 26 din mein Sunday nahi gine jaate, toh ₹15,000 par ek din ₹576.92 ka hota hai, 30 din mein ₹500 ka. Jo bhi chuno, saare staff ke liye har mahine wahi rakho.

**Q: Half day ki salary kaise nikale?**

A: Ek din ki salary ka aadha kaato. ₹15,000 salary aur 31 din ke mahine mein ek din ₹483.87 ka hai, toh half day par ₹241.94 katega. 26 din wale tarike mein yahi ₹288.46 hoga.

**Q: Daily wage ki salary kaise calculate kare?**

A: Paid din ko din ke rate se guna karo. Paid din matlab kaam ke din plus approved paid leave, jaise 25 din × ₹550 = ₹13,750. Sunday aur chhutti ka paisa dena hai ya nahi, yeh state ke niyam par depend karta hai, CA se poochho.

**Q: Piece rate salary kaise nikale?**

A: Har kaam ka rate tay karo aur roz ke pieces likho, phir mahine ke pieces ko rate se guna karo. 420 dozen × ₹18 = ₹7,560. Do kaam hon toh dono ki rakam jod do.
