## Does the salesman need internet to check in at a shop?

Station Road at 10:40. Gupta Stores sits in a lane where 4G dies behind the concrete. Raju still opens the party and checks in. The camera opens. Gallery is not an option. He photographs the shutter. ## Saved on this phone, on Station Road

Saved on this phone is the label Raju sees. The visit is his, on this handset, until the radio comes back. He can check out in the same lane. Check-out is saved the same way. Duration and distance wait with the two stamps, including "At the dealer 12 minutes" and "Checked out 40 m from where the visit started," and they upload together.

He does not re-take the photo when signal returns. He does not type the visit again at 9 PM. The upload is the same record, the camera photo, the coordinates, the times. If the phone reported a mock location, check-in was already refused on the device. Signal does not soften that. The refused attempt still shows to the owner once it can travel.

Five salesmen means five handsets. Manoj on GS Road can be offline in a different pocket at the same hour. The owner's feed stays empty for those visits until each phone finds data.

Visit capture is phone-only. Field Visits is available on request. The owner's Field Visits screen is phone-only. Feed defaults to today, filterable by salesman or Everyone. Until Raju's phone uploads, Gupta Stores is not on that feed. After the upload it is, with the caption plate: salesman, approximate street address prefixed "Approx.", coordinates with accuracy, timestamp.

## What an offline field visit app will not do

An offline field visit app, here, will not raise a Sales Order in a dead-signal lane. It will not raise an invoice. It will not raise a receipt. Creating an order, invoice or receipt needs a working data connection.

That limit is the one distributors trip over, because the market word "offline" usually means the whole day queues and syncs later. This product queues visits, and vouchers wait for signal. Raju cannot punch 14 cartons and trust the phone to post them at 4G. He has to move to where there is signal before an order, invoice or receipt goes through.

On ordinary 4G in a market street it works normally. The dead-signal pocket is the exception the two sentences are for. Station Road has two such pockets. NH-37 has one under the flyover.

## Take an order after check-out, once there is signal

Take an order is offered after check-out. Sales orders need signal. The sequence on a good lane is check-in, the conversation, check-out, then the order against live stock. The sequence in a dead lane is check-in, the conversation, check-out, walk to where there are bars, then the order.

At 10:52 Raju has walked twenty metres toward Station Road's main stretch. Signal returns. The visit that said Saved on this phone uploads. He books 14 cartons. It lands in Tally as an ordinary Sales Order, which is the point of [salesman order taking without re-entry](/blog/salesman-order-to-tally-without-reentry/). He can raise an order any other time. Order creation is not gated on Team Sales.

If he tries to book before he has data, the order does not queue. He waits. Gupta is used to that. "Signal aate hi likh dena."

Live stock is why the order needs a connection. The godown figure has to be the current one, not a figure the phone remembered at 9 AM. Visits do not promise cartons. They can wait.

The [dispatch load sheet](/blog/dispatch-load-sheet-for-distributors/) still totals pending orders from a 3-day window once those orders exist in Tally. An order that never left the dead lane never reaches the sheet.

## What the owner sees after the upload

The owner does not watch Raju's radio. He opens Field Visits after 8 PM. Gupta Stores is there, 10:40, the photo, the plate. If Raju is still in the pocket, the row is not there yet. "Photo upload hua?" is a smaller call than "gaya tha ya nahi."

Mock location is still refused on the device. Signal returning does not post a fake visit. If the owner has granted that device an exemption, the visit is accepted and marked Unverified, and that label survives the upload. The trust check is the mock-location flag, on the phone, at the moment of the stamp.

A Tuesday with two dead-signal pockets will show those visits late, in a cluster, when Raju hits the main road. The timestamps stay 10:40 and 10:52. The upload time is not the visit time. Device time is.
