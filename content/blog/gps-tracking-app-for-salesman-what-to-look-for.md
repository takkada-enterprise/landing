## Mock-location honesty before you buy

Ask what the app does when the phone reports a mock or fake location.

The honest answer is refusal. Check-in is refused. No visit is recorded. The refused attempt still shows to the owner. He opens Raju, today, and sees that Gupta Stores was tried with a mock location. He WhatsApps at 8:10. "Gupta Stores ka visit nahi aaya, mock location dikha raha hai."

[Fake GPS on a salesman's phone](/blog/fake-gps-location-field-sales-what-happens/) is that flag, on the device, at the moment of check-in. The trust check is the mock-location flag only. He can check in when he is not at the shop, if the flag is off. Honesty is naming that limit in the demo.

Some devices report a mock location when the salesman is not faking anything. Older phones, a leftover developer setting. An owner can grant that device an exemption. The visit is then accepted and marked Unverified. Unverified means the location could not be trusted the usual way. The photo, the name and the time still stand.
