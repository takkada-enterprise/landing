## What Automatic Dispatch Actually Removes

The manual version of this job is small and endless. Save the invoice, export the PDF, open WhatsApp on the office phone, find the retailer, attach, type "invoice bheji hai," send, move to the next one. At ten invoices a day it is a quarter hour. At fifty it is well over an hour, every single day, and it is not skilled work.

Automatic dispatch deletes the whole sequence. The accountant records the sale in Tally and moves to the next entry. The invoice leaves on its own, to the right number, before they have finished the next voucher. There is no queue of invoices waiting for someone to get to the forwarding, because there is no forwarding.

The [existing walkthrough of Tally-to-WhatsApp dispatch](/blog/tally-whatsapp-invoice-dispatch/) covers the mechanics in depth. The point here is narrower: once it is automatic, the task stops existing.

## Why the Timing Beats the Time Saved

The hour saved is nice. The timing is the real prize.

A retailer's payment clock runs from when they receive the invoice, not from when you saved it. Manual forwarding builds in a delay of hours, and a Saturday batch that reaches the retailer on Monday builds in days. An invoice on 30-day credit that is delivered three days late is effectively collected on day 33 before anyone is even late.

Automatic dispatch starts the clock at the moment of creation. Save at 2 PM, delivered at 2 PM. Over 300 invoices a month, closing that gap on every one of them pulls the whole receivables curve earlier, which is the same [days sales outstanding](/blog/days-sales-outstanding-distributor-india/) drag that manual sharing quietly adds.

## What Rides Along With the Invoice

A dispatched invoice carries more than a PDF. It brings the details that make it easy to act on, and a UPI link for the exact amount, so the retailer sees the bill and can pay it in the same message on a [0% MDR UPI rail](/blog/zero-mdr-upi-collection-for-distributors-india/). The gap between "received the invoice" and "made the payment" collapses to one tap.

For a distributor who invoices from the field, the invoice can be the [GST-compliant one with the e-invoice IRN and e-way bill](/blog/e-invoice-on-phone-tally/) already stamped on it. The compliant document reaches the retailer before the goods are even unloaded, and disputes over rate or quantity surface within hours instead of on day 28.

## How It Stays a Tally Invoice, Not a Copy

The invoice that dispatches is your Tally invoice. Nothing is re-keyed into a separate system and nothing drifts from the book of record. The dispatch layer reads the saved voucher and the party's contact from Tally and sends it, and the sale stays exactly where the accountant expects it, in Tally, over a [two-way sync](/blog/bidirectional-tally-sync-explained/).

So the owner does not trade reliability for speed. The invoice is the same document the print template produces, delivered automatically instead of by hand, with Tally still the single source everyone trusts.
