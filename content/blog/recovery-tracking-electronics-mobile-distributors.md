## Short credit, big bills, thin margins

A typical mobile and electronics distributor supplies 100 to 300 retailers on 7 to 30 day credit. One launch week can put ₹4 lakh on a single large retailer's account. Margins sit in low single digits, so a payment that slips a fortnight eats into the profit on that stock.

The delays usually have a reason attached. The retailer is waiting for a price protection credit after a price cut, a scheme payout he expected, or a replacement for a unit that failed on day one. Your salesman hears one version, your accountant hears another, and neither writes it down.

## Writing down the reason and the date

The Log follow-up sheet takes Call, WhatsApp, In person or Other, and one outcome: Will pay, No answer, Wrong number, Disputes amount, Refused or Other. A promise is an amount and a date together, so a post-dated cheque goes in as its amount and the cheque date. The note carries the detail, like "price drop credit on 12 units pending".

Already tried shows the last five entries on that retailer, each naming who logged it. Before the salesman walks into the shop, he knows the office called yesterday and what the retailer said.

## The morning list

The daily brief sits at the top of the Recovery screen in the Collect tab. Promises due today shows which retailers committed to pay today. Follow-ups due shows the ones you planned to call back. Missed promises lists retailers whose promised date passed with no receipt in your Tally books up to that date. Top overdue lists the biggest balances.

If Takkada's copy of your Tally data has not caught up past a promised date, the promise sits under Awaiting confirmation instead of missed. A Share on WhatsApp button sends the brief's text to your sales group.

## Who is chasing which retailer

Each retailer on the Parties tab shows the person chasing it, taken from whoever last logged a follow-up, or Not yet chased. A company admin can reassign a retailer. The Team tab shows Recovered, Still out, Dealers worked and Promises kept, then each person's figures, and a Nobody has chased line for overdue retailers no one has touched.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I log a post-dated cheque?**

A: Save it as a promise with the cheque amount and the cheque date. After that date, Takkada checks for the receipt in your Tally books.

**Q: Can I record that a retailer is waiting for a price-drop credit?**

A: Yes. Pick Disputes amount and write the reason in the note. Everyone who opens that retailer sees it.

**Q: Does a part payment show up?**

A: If receipts entered in Tally up to the promised date cover part of the promise, it shows Part paid.

**Q: Does Takkada edit my Tally data?**

A: No. Follow-ups and promises stay in Takkada, and your Tally receipts are only read to check each promise.
