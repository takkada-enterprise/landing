To start AI collection calls in Takkada, open Payment Reminders, tap Send reminders now, tick the overdue parties you want called, tap Call with AI, check the confirm sheet and tap Call now. The calls go out shortly after, and each result comes back as a follow-up on that party. Before your first round, spend two minutes on the AI calling screen under Payment Reminders, where you set the calling hours, the calling days, the language, the tone, the monthly cap per party and whether a missed promise gets an automatic re-call. You need Smart Reminders and AI calling switched on for your business; if the AI calling row does not appear under Payment Reminders, AI calling is not on yet. This guide walks through each step with the button names exactly as they appear on the screen, and shows what you will see after the calls, including who was skipped and why, and where the outcome, promise, note and recording show up.

## Step 1: Open the AI calling settings

Go to Payment Reminders. Below the reminder history row you will see **AI calling**, with the line "Automatic re-calls, calling hours, language and limits". Tap it.

## Step 2: Set the rules

The AI calling screen has seven settings:

1. **Call again when a promise is missed.** On by default. If a party does not pay what they promised, the AI calls them again.
2. **Days after the promised date.** How long to wait before that re-call. The default is 1.
3. **Start calling from** and **Stop calling at.** The default window is 10:00 to 18:00.
4. **Calling days.** Chips from Mon to Sun. Monday to Saturday is the default.
5. **Max AI calls per party per month.** Anywhere from 1 to 31. The default is 4.
6. **Language.** One of twelve: Hindi, English, Assamese, Bengali, Odia, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam. Hindi is the default.
7. **Tone**, Polite or Firm, and **Number the party should call back (optional).** Leave the number empty to use the owner's number.

Tap **Save Settings**.

## Step 3: Open Send reminders now

Back on Payment Reminders, tap **Send reminders now** ("Chase selected customers straight away"). The screen lists every party that owes money, with the overdue total, the number of overdue bills and the age of the oldest bill. The total matches your Bills Receivable screen.

## Step 4: Pick the parties

Search by name, or use the ledger group chips to narrow the list to one Tally group. Tick parties one by one, or use **Select all**, which only reaches the rows already loaded on screen. A party with no mobile number shows "No mobile number saved" and cannot be ticked.

## Step 5: Tap Call with AI

At the bottom of the screen, next to **Send now**, tap **Call with AI**. A sheet opens titled "Call 12 parties with AI", with the cost shown before you commit. Tap **Call now**.

## Step 6: Read the result sheet

After a short "Setting up 12 calls…" step, the sheet says "Queued 11 calls" and lists every party that was not queued, by name, with the reason: no phone number on the ledger, asked not to be called, a call already queued, nothing overdue, not in your ledger scope, or monthly AI call limit reached. Parties that were not queued stay ticked, so a retry needs no re-selection.

## Step 7: Check the results

With the Recovery dashboard on, each call lands on the party's follow-up timeline, tagged AI, with the outcome, the note and a Play recording button, and Recovery's History tab shows any promise with its amount and date. Without it, open the party's bills page and tap **AI call results**.
