Spare parts ka rate cost + margin se banane ka seedha tarika hai: part ki last purchase cost lo aur us par apna markup lagao, aur Takkada ka AutoParts billing yahi hisaab phone par khud karta hai. Cost aati hai us part ke latest purchase bill se, line ka discount ghata ke, GST se pehle, part ki apni unit mein. Markup aap teen jagah set kar sakte ho: poori company ke liye, kisi stock group ke liye, ya ek item ke liye. Item ka apna markup sabse pehle, phir group ka, phir company ka. Ek bearing ₹250.24 mein aaya aur group ka markup 40% hai, to rate bana ₹350.34, GST alag se bill par judega. Agla purchase bill naye cost par app mein aaya, to rate bhi naye cost se banega. Counter wale ko sirf selling rate dikhta hai, cost aur markup unhe nahi dikhte jinhe purchase cost dekhne ki permission nahi hai.

## Dukaan ka asli scene

Aapke paas teen-chaar brand ke hazaaron part number hain. Garage wale, mistri aur chhoti dukaanein 15 se 30 din ke udhaar par maal le jaate hain. Rate ki ek purani list hai, Excel mein ya diary mein, jo last baar do mahine pehle update hui thi.

Company ne rate badhaya, naya maal mehenga aaya, lekin counter pe purana rate chal raha hai. Mahine ke end mein hisaab dekho to margin kam nikla. List ko roz update karna kisi ke bas ki baat nahi hai, isliye counter purane rate par chalta rehta hai.

## Margin aur markup ka farak

Yahan ek baat saaf kar lo. App mein jo percentage aap daalte ho, woh cost ke upar lagta hai, isliye screen par uska naam Markup % hai.

- Markup 40%: ₹250.24 × 1.40 = ₹350.34
- Agar 40% margin selling price par chahiye hota, to rate ₹417.07 banta

Dono mein ₹66.73 ka farak hai. Aap jo "margin" bolte ho, agar woh cost par lagta hai, to app ka Markup % wahi hai. Rate do decimal tak round hota hai.

## Setting kahan hai

Settings, Invoices, AutoParts billing kholo. Upar Company default markup hai, jaise 30%. Neeche Stock groups ki list hai, jo Tally ke stock groups se aati hai. Bearings par 40%, Oil seals par 55% daal do. Kisi ek part ko alag rate chahiye to group kholo aur Set item markup karo. Kisi part ko purane rate par hi rakhna hai to Exclude from AutoParts billing karo, woh apne normal rate par bikega.

Markup sirf business admin badal sakta hai. Baaki log, jinhe purchase cost dekhne ki permission hai, sirf dekh sakte hain.

## Bill par kya aata hai

Sales invoice, quotation ya sales order mein part daalo, rate apne aap aa jaata hai. Ek misaal:

- 10 bearing × ₹350.34 = ₹3,503.40 taxable
- 18% GST par CGST ₹315.31 aur SGST ₹315.31
- Total ₹4,134.02

Doosri misaal: ₹118.75 cost wala clutch cable, 30% markup par ₹154.38. 12 piece ka taxable ₹1,852.56, CGST ₹166.73, SGST ₹166.73, total ₹2,186.02.

Customer ne mol-bhav kiya aur aapne line par rate badal diya, to line par wahi rate rahega. Bill Tally mein normal sales voucher ki tarah jaata hai, line ke rate ke saath. Cost aur markup app mein hi rehte hain.

## Jab rate nahi banta

Kuch part ka rate cost se nahi banta. Jo part kabhi khareeda hi nahi, Stock Summary mein uske aage Never purchased likha aata hai. Agar purchase alag unit mein book hua, jaise item Nos mein hai aur purchase Box mein, to woh purchase ginti mein nahi aata, aur No usable cost dikhta hai. Aise part apne normal rate par bikte hain jab tak sahi unit mein purchase na aaye.

## Naya maal aaya, rate kitna badla

AutoParts billing screen par Price changes dabao. Pichhle 90 din ke purchase bill ek-ek card mein dikhte hain. Card ke andar part ka purana aur naya cost, kitne percent badla, aur selling rate pehle aur ab, aaj ke markup se. 10% se zyada badle part alag se highlight hote hain.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Spare parts ka rate kis purchase se banta hai?**

A: Part ke latest purchase bill se, discount ghata ke, GST se pehle. Preferred supplier set kiya ho to us supplier ke latest bill se, aur uska koi bill na ho to kisi ke bhi latest bill se.

**Q: Kya purana bill bhi naye rate se badal jaayega?**

A: Nahi. Save hua bill apne rate par rehta hai, us line par cost aur markup bhi save rehta hai.

**Q: Salesman ko meri cost dikhegi?**

A: Tabhi jab use purchase cost dekhne ki permission ho. Warna use sirf selling rate dikhta hai.
