## Bakaya kaise vasool kare: pehle problem samajho, baad mein tool

Har distributor ke paas ek hi sawaal hota hai mahine ke aakhir mein: "bhai paisa kab aayega". Maal toh nikal gaya, invoice bhi chala gaya, par bakaya 60-70 din latka rehta hai. Bakaya kaise vasool kare ka jawab koi ek WhatsApp message nahi hai. Ye ek system hai jo har party pe roz chalna chahiye, bina aapke daily call kiye.

Pehle samjho problem badi kyun ho gayi. Aaj ek retailer 7 se 12 distributor se maal uthata hai. Sabka udhaar chal raha hai. Aapka invoice us retailer ke dimaag mein ek line bhi nahi, dozen mein se ek hai. Jiska reminder strong, jiska dispatch hold sakht, wahi pehle paisa leta hai. Baaki "baad mein dekh lenge" wali list mein.

Doosri baat, margin pehle se patla hai. 3 se 5% pe kaam chal raha hai, aur usi mein 65 din ka bakaya floating rakhna padta hai. Matlab aap apna stock bhi fund kar rahe ho aur retailer ka purchase cycle bhi. Isliye bakaya vasool karna ab "thoda late ho gaya" wali baat nahi, business chalne ya band hone ki baat hai.

Teesri baat, UPI aane se collection asaan hua par reconciliation ka naya sirdard aa gaya. Accountant ab cash nahi ginta, bank statement leke baitha rehta hai aur 40-80 UTR ko invoice se haath se match karta hai. Galti hoti hai, ledger galat dikhta hai, aur jiska paisa aa chuka usko bhi reminder chala jaata hai. Ye sab thik karna hi asli vasuli hai.

## Ledger bhejna kaam kyun nahi karta, aur kya karta hai

Sabse common galti: month-end pe pura partywise ledger ki photo kheench ke bhej dena. "Ji aapka ₹2,40,000 baaki hai, dekh lijiye". Retailer us screenshot ko khol ke nahi dekhta. Usko samajh nahi aata kaunsa invoice, kaunsi date, kitne din late. Bas ek dabav wala message lagta hai jise woh ignore kar deta hai.

Jo cheez kaam karti hai woh ulta hai. Ek invoice, ek amount, ek due date, aur saath mein ek UPI link jisko dabane se paisa direct chala jaata hai. "Bhai invoice #1247, ₹14,320, 20 tareekh ko due. Link se bhej do, do minute ka kaam hai." Retailer ke liye link dabana 5 second ka kaam hai, IFSC daal ke NEFT karna 5 minute ka. Behaviour simple hai. Jo asaan hai woh pehle pay hota hai.

Iska matlab ye nahi ki ledger kabhi nahi bhejna. Jab koi party 60 din paar kar gayi aur poora hisaab manga rahi hai, tab ledger statement kaam ka hai. Tally se nikla saaf partywise statement bhejna alag baat hai. Isko hum ne yahan detail mein likha hai: [partywise outstanding statement Tally](/blog/partywise-outstanding-statement-tally/). Par routine vasuli ke liye ledger blast band karo, single-invoice reminder shuru karo.

## Reminder cadence: kab kya message bhejna hai

Bakaya vasool karne ka asli engine cadence hai. Fixed dino pe, fixed message, har invoice pe alag. Manual call par mat depend raho, kyunki call tab miss hota hai jab accountant busy hai, salesman field mein hai, aur owner travel pe. Cadence khud chalti rehti hai.

| Din | Message ka mizaaj | Kya bhejna hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 0 | Invoice dispatch | Invoice + UPI link, WhatsApp pe turant |
| Day 3 | Halka reminder | "Maal mil gaya na? Invoice #1247, ₹14,320" |
| Day 15 | Due-date notice | "Bhai kal due hai, link se settle kar do" |
| Day 30 | Sakht reminder | "Baaki clear karo, agla dispatch hold ho sakta hai" |
| Day 45 | Owner escalation | Owner khud baat kare, accountant nahi |

Har message specific invoice aur amount ka naam le. Koi bhi message poora ledger blast na ho. Aur sabse zaroori, jis pal invoice ka paisa aa gaya, us invoice pe cadence apne aap ruk jaaye. Warna paid party ko reminder jaata rehta hai aur rishta kharab hota hai. Isi cadence ko hum ne step-by-step yahan kholaa hai: [WhatsApp payment reminder for distributors](/blog/whatsapp-payment-reminder-for-distributors/).

Ek baat jo Tier 2/3 mein bahut farak laati hai. Language wahi rakho jo retailer aapse bolta hai. Hinglish North aur Central India mein bharosa banata hai. Formal English reminder bank recovery notice jaisa lagta hai aur ignore ho jaata hai.

## UPI link aur reconciliation: paisa aaya to khud Tally mein chadhe

Sirf link bhej dena aadha kaam hai. Acha collection system char cheezein karta hai link bhejne ke baad:

Link ke andar ek reference daalta hai jo us payment ko us hi invoice se baandh deta hai. Distributor ke bank account pe nazar rakhta hai ki us reference wala UTR aaya ki nahi. UTR aate hi khud Tally mein us invoice ke against receipt voucher chadha deta hai. Aur us invoice pe reminder cadence apne aap rok deta hai.

Yahi woh jagah hai jahan zyada distributor phaste hain. Paisa aa gaya bank mein, par Tally mein chadha nahi, to outstanding report jhooth bolti hai. Jiska paisa aa chuka usko reminder chala jaata hai, aur jiska nahi aaya woh kabhi-kabhi list se gayab. Manual reconciliation jitni der lagti hai, utni der aapka bakaya galat dikhta hai.

## Credit limit: bakaya badhne se pehle roko

Sabse durable vasuli woh hai jo invoice banne se pehle hoti hai. Matlab har party ka ek credit limit fix ho, yaani maximum kitna bakaya kabhi bhi rahega. Ye retailer ki demand pe nahi, uske do-teen mahine ke purchase volume pe tay karo.

Ek simple rule jo kayi distributor use karte hain: agar kisi party ka bakaya uske average monthly purchase ke 2 guna se zyada ho gaya, to nayi credit ruk jaaye jab tak settlement na ho. Isse "maal rokna hai ki nahi" wala decision rishte pe nahi, number pe based ho jaata hai. Credit limit kaise set karein iska poora template yahan hai: [credit limit for retailers](/blog/credit-limit-for-retailers/).

Jo distributor credit limit clear rakhte hain unka bakaya kabhi out of control nahi jaata. Jo nahi rakhte, woh saal mein 2-4 party ko ₹3-5 lakh ke bad debt tak le jaate hain. 3% margin pe woh recover karne mein mahine lag jaate hain. Bakaya kaise vasool kare ka aadha jawab to yahi hai: usse pehle hi cap laga do.

## Dispatch hold: jab baat se kaam na ho

Cadence bina consequence ke ek polite request hai. Retailer ko pata chal jaata hai kaunsa distributor sakht hai aur kaunsa ignore kiya ja sakta hai. Isliye 45-60 din pe ek line honi chahiye.

Day 30 ka message saaf bole: "agla dispatch hold hoga agar 7 din mein clear nahi hua." Day 45 pe owner khud bole ki ab consequence active hai. Aur Day 60 pe dispatch sach mein roko jab tak clearance na ho. Family-run distributor ke liye yahi sabse mushkil step hai. Rishta purana hai, retailer push karta hai, aur owner aksar piche hat jaata hai.

Par jo distributor consistently dispatch hold karte hain, unki receivables book sabse healthy chalti hai. Ek Surat textile distributor mahine mein 2-3 party ko is rule pe hold karta hai aur phir bhi 18% saal-dar-saal badha, kyunki jo retailer rishta value karte the woh supply chalu rakhne ke liye time pe pay kar dete the. Sakhti rishta nahi todti, woh paani saaf rakhti hai.

Aur jo party 60 din paar karke bhi cadence ignore kar rahi hai, teen raaste hain: cash-and-carry pe daal do (ab UPI pehle, maal baad mein), weekly tranche ka written plan banao WhatsApp pe, ya bechna band karo. Saal mein 2-4 party aisi nikalti hain. ₹3-5 lakh ka bad debt dhone se acha hai us ek sale ko chhod dena.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Bakaya kaise vasool kare bina rishta kharab kiye?**

A: Specific raho, blast mat karo. Ek invoice, ek amount, ek due date, saath mein UPI link. Ye reminder service lagta hai, demand nahi. Jo rishta kharab karta hai woh hai vague calls, poora ledger screenshot, aur paid party ko bhi reminder jaate rehna. Cadence ko paid hote hi rok do, to retailer ko lagta hai aap organised ho, dushman nahi.

**Q: Ledger bhej diya hai phir bhi paisa nahi aaya, ab kya karu?**

A: Ledger blast aam tor pe ignore hota hai. Us party ke sabse purane single invoice ko utha ke uska reminder bhejo amount aur due date ke saath, ek UPI link daalo. Agar 30 din paar ho gaye to message mein dispatch hold ki line saaf likho. 60 din paar to owner khud call kare, accountant nahi.

**Q: Choti-choti party ka bakaya vasool karne mein time bahut lagta hai, kya karu?**

A: Yahi pe automation kaam aata hai. 50-300 party ka manual reminder insaan se nahi hota, koi na koi chhoot jaata hai. Fixed cadence (Day 3, 15, 30, 45) khud chalti rahe har invoice pe, to chhoti party bhi miss nahi hoti aur aapka time bachta hai. App un sab ko ek saath handle karta hai, aap sirf hold/escalate wale exception dekhte ho.

**Q: UPI se bakaya lene pe koi charge katta hai kya?**

A: Bahut payment apps katte hain, ₹3 per txn ya percentage MDR. Takkada pe nahi: 0% MDR on UPI collections, no transaction cap, no monthly fee. Jitna bakaya aaya, pura aapke khaate mein. 300 collection ka mahina ho to ye bachat saaf dikhti hai.

**Q: Paisa aane ke baad Tally mein receipt khud chadhega ya manual karna padega?**

A: Acha system khud chadhata hai. UPI link mein invoice ka reference hota hai, paisa aate hi us invoice ke against receipt voucher Tally mein chadh jaata hai aur reminder ruk jaata hai. Iska matlab outstanding report sach bolti hai aur 9 baje wala manual reconciliation gayab ho jaata hai.

**Q: Bakaya badhne se pehle kaise roku?**

A: Har party ka credit limit fix karo. Rule simple hai: bakaya agar uske average monthly purchase ke 2 guna se upar gaya to nayi credit ruk jaaye. Number pe decision lo, rishte pe nahi.
