Beat plan kya hota hai, seedhi bhasha mein: dealers ki ek fixed list (beat) aur yeh tay karna ki hafte ke kis din kaun salesman us beat par jayega. Plan us beat ko hafte ke dino aur salesmen se jodta hai. Guwahati ke ek distributor ka example lo, jiske paas teen salesman hain: Raju, Manoj aur Amit, aur paanch beat: Station Road, GS Road, Fancy Bazaar, NH-37 aur Beltola. Station Road somvaar aur guruvaar ko chalta hai, aur mangalvaar aur shukravaar ko bhi, par alag salesman ke saath. Somvaar ko Raju jaata hai, mangalvaar ko Amit. Is tarah ek beat par alag din alag salesman ja sakta hai, aur har salesman ko subah pata hota hai ki aaj kaunsi dukaanein hain. Trade mein aam taur par ek beat mein 20 se 30 dealer rakhe jaate hain, jitne ek salesman ek din mein cover kar le. Jis beat par koi din tick nahi, woh roz chalta hai.

## Beat, route aur journey plan mein fark

Teeno shabd field sales mein ek saath bole jaate hain, isliye confusion hota hai.

| Shabd | Matlab | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Beat | Ek ilaake ke dealers ki fixed list | Station Road ke 24 kirana |
| Route | Woh rasta jis par salesman ya gaadi chalti hai | Paltan Bazaar se Station Road hote hue Athgaon |
| Journey plan / beat plan | Kis din kaun salesman kaunsa beat karega | Somvaar Raju Station Road, mangalvaar Amit Station Road |

Zyadatar distributor beat aur route ko ek hi cheez bolte hain, aur usme koi galti nahi. Beat ki pehchaan dukaanon ki list se hai. Plan ki pehchaan din aur salesman se hai. Beat kya hota hai, iski poori baat English mein [what is a beat in distribution sales](/blog/what-is-a-beat-in-distribution-sales/) wale article mein hai.

## Ek beat mein kitne dealer

Trade mein aam taur par ek salesman ek din mein 20 se 30 dealer cover karta hai, scooter par, shehar ke andar. Isliye beat bhi utna hi bada rakha jaata hai. Agar Station Road par 24 dukaanein hain, toh woh ek din ka beat hai. Agar kisi ilaake mein 50 dukaanein hain, toh use do beat mein todo, jaise Fancy Bazaar 1 aur Fancy Bazaar 2. Gaon ya highway beat par dukaanein door-door hoti hain, wahan kam dukaanein bhi poora din le leti hain.

## Hafte ka beat plan: example table

Teen salesman aur paanch beat ka ek hafta aisa dikh sakta hai:

| Salesman | Som | Mangal | Budh | Guru | Shukra | Shani |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raju | Station Road | Fancy Bazaar | NH-37 | Station Road | Fancy Bazaar | NH-37 |
| Manoj | GS Road | Beltola | GS Road | Beltola | GS Road | Beltola |
| Amit | Fancy Bazaar | Station Road | Fancy Bazaar | NH-37 | Station Road | Chhutti |

Is table se teen baatein seedhi dikhti hain. Station Road hafte mein chaar baar jaata hai, do baar Raju ke saath aur do baar Amit ke saath. Fancy Bazaar bhi chaar baar jaata hai, kyunki wahan wholesale dukaanein zyada hain aur order roz aata hai. Aur shanivaar ko Amit ki chhutti hai, toh us din uska koi beat nahi.

## Ek beat, alag din, alag salesman

Pehle zyadatar distributor ek beat par ek hi salesman rakhte the. Problem tab aati hai jab ek beat hafte mein do baar chalna chahiye aur ek salesman ke paas utne din nahi. Ya jab ek salesman ki chhutti hai aur uska beat kisi aur ko dena hai. Din-wise plan mein aap beat par din tick karte ho aur har din ka salesman alag chunte ho. Station Road somvaar ko Raju, mangalvaar ko Amit. Dukaanon ki list wahi rehti hai, sirf jaane wala badalta hai.

Isse dukaandar ko bhi fayda hai. Usse pata hota hai ki somvaar aur mangalvaar ko salesman aayega, toh woh order us din ke liye rakhta hai.

## Jis beat par koi din tick nahi

Har beat ko din-wise banana zaroori nahi. Jis beat par aapne koi din tick nahi kiya, woh roz chalta hai, bilkul pehle jaisa. Toh jo team pehle se beats use kar rahi hai, uske liye kuch nahi badalta. Jab zaroorat ho, ek-ek beat par din tick karte jao.
