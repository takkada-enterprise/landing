Receivables management software for Tally is a mobile-first application layer that enables business owners and finance teams to track unpaid customer invoices, automate WhatsApp payment reminders, collect zero-MDR UPI payments, and reconcile receipts back into Tally. Operating directly on top of existing desktop Tally Prime or Tally ERP 9 installations, receivables management software extends accounting visibility to mobile devices without requiring desktop access or manual spreadsheet exports. Distributors and wholesalers can view real-time party-wise outstanding balances, inspect aging slabs, share ledger statements on WhatsApp, and schedule automated payment follow-ups around invoice due dates. When retail buyers pay via attached UPI payment links, the software automatically allocates the received amount against open sales invoices—handling full, partial, or multi-bill settlements—and posts receipt entries into Tally. This replaces manual diary tracking and late-night bank statement matching with a single connected receivables workflow from phone to accounting books.

Takkada is receivables management software built for businesses that run their accounts on Tally.

It gives business owners and teams a single workflow to see outstanding payments, follow up with customers, collect money and reconcile payments back into Tally.

## What does receivables management software do?

Receivables management software helps businesses manage money that customers still owe them.

A typical receivables workflow includes:

1. Identify outstanding invoices in real time.
2. Check how long each invoice has been unpaid (aging analysis).
3. Contact the customer with payment details.
4. Send payment information and zero-MDR UPI payment links.
5. Collect the payment digitally.
6. Match the payment with the corresponding invoice.
7. Update Tally accounting records automatically.

Takkada brings these steps together around your Tally installation.
