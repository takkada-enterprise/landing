## Visit duration tracking in field sales when check-out cannot be trusted

Visit duration tracking in field sales has a second honesty rule, and it is the one that keeps the twelve-minute rows believable.

Check-out runs its own trust check. The trust check is the mock-location flag, the same flag check-in uses. If the phone reports a mock or fake location on the way out, the check-out leg fails. Duration is then shown as unreliable. The minutes are still computed. They are not presented as a clean "At the dealer 12 minutes" the owner can take to a review.

If check-in was refused because of a mock location, there is no visit at all, so there is no duration. The refused attempt still shows to the owner. If the owner has granted that device an exemption, the visit is accepted and marked Unverified. Unverified is about the location. Unreliable duration is about the check-out leg.

A visit still open at 8 PM is its own signal. Manoj on GS Road, if he checks in at nine shops and checks out of six, leaves three open. The owner sees the open ones.
