## Tally Mobile Par Kaise Chalaye: Asli Matlab Kya Hai

Tally mobile par kaise chalaye ye sawaal galat tab ho jaata hai jab aap sochte hain ki Play Store se "Tally" download karke login kar lenge. Tally Prime ek Windows desktop software hai. Aapki company file office ki ek machine pe rehti hai, aur koi official app phone pe khud Tally nahi chalata. Isliye phone pe Tally chalane ka matlab hamesha ek bridge banana hai, aapke phone se us office machine tak.

Ek tarah ka bridge sirf number dikhata hai. Doosra bridge aapko un numbers pe kaam karne deta hai. Ek distributor jiska din 18 se 30 retail party ke route pe chalta hai, uske liye ye farak tay karta hai ki phone ek khidki hai ya ek poora workspace. Agar aapko background chahiye ki native client kyun nahi hai, to [Tally ko mobile pe access karne wali full guide](/blog/how-to-access-tally-on-mobile-step-by-step/) pehle padh lein. Aage hum companion app wala raasta step by step kholte hain, kyunki distributor ke liye yahi practical hai.

## Step 1: Pehle Apni Zaroorat Tay Karein

Kuch bhi install karne se pehle decide karein ki aapko phone se kya karwana hai:

- **Sirf number dekhne hain** (outstanding, ledger, stock): ek view-only companion app kaafi hai
- **Field se invoice banani hai aur paisa collect karna hai**: aapko read-and-write companion app chahiye
- **CA ko thodi der ke liye access dena hai**: Tally ka apna Tally.NET remote feature isi ke liye bana hai

Pehle tay karne se aap teen tool install karke ek bhi use na karne wali galti se bach jaate hain. Ek family-run distributor jo sirf "is party ka kitna baaki hai" janna chahta hai, uska setup ek collections-heavy distributor se bilkul alag hota hai.

## Step 2: Companion App Ka Connector Office Tally Pe Lagayein

Yahi wo step hai jahan se phone pe asli Tally experience shuru hota hai. Tally phone pe kaise chalaye iska core yahi connector hai.

1. Vendor ka chhota connector ya agent us office Windows machine pe install karein jahan Tally chalti hai
2. Connector aapki Tally se data Tally XML gateway ke through padhta hai. Ye Tally ka built-in channel hai jisse wo doosre programs ko data deta hai
3. Connector ek copy app ke cloud pe sync kar deta hai, taaki phone pe sab kuch khul jaaye
4. Office PC ko on aur signed-in rakhein, taaki sync chalta rahe

Yahan ek baat saaf samajh lein. Koi extra Tally licence nahi lagta. Connector aapki maujooda Tally installation se hi judta hai. Office machine band ho jaaye to phone pe cached data dikhta rehta hai aur aapke likhe entries queue mein ruk jaate hain, PC wapas aane pe sync ho jaata hai. Is connector ki safety ke sawaal par hum alag se [app ko Tally se jodna safe hai ya nahi](/blog/is-it-safe-to-connect-app-to-tally/) wali guide mein detail dete hain.

## Step 3: Phone Pe Ledger Aur Outstanding Kaise Dekhein

Connector lag gaya, to ab phone app mein login karte hi data aa jaata hai.

1. Phone app install karein aur apni company se login karein
2. Pehle screen pe hi outstanding, day book, aur top parties dikhne lagte hain
3. Kisi party pe tap karein aur uska poora ledger mobile pe khulega, har voucher ke saath
4. Stock aur reports bhi yahin se dekh sakte hain, office machine chhuye bina

Itne se hi bahut owners ke liye kaam ho jaata hai. Field mein khade-khade aap dekh lete hain ki ek retailer ₹1,40,000 baaki rakhe baitha hai, aur salesman ko fresh credit dene se rok dete hain. Agar aapko ye samajhna hai ki phone pe kaun se reports sabse kaam ke hain, to [Tally reports mobile pe dekhne](/blog/view-tally-reports-on-mobile/) wali guide ye view-only side ko khol deti hai. Aur party-wise baaki ka ek saaf statement chahiye to [party-wise outstanding statement](/blog/partywise-outstanding-statement-tally/) wala article wahi flow dikhata hai.

## Step 4: Phone Se Invoice Aur UPI Vasooli Kaise Karein

Ye wo step hai jo poora din badal deta hai, sirf view nahi.

1. Connector wahi rehta hai jo Step 2 mein laga tha, magar ab sync dono taraf chalta hai
2. Phone pe aap sales invoice banate hain (e-invoice aur e-way bill ke saath), aur wo wapas office Tally mein post ho jaati hai
3. Har invoice ke saath ek UPI payment link jaata hai jo PhonePe, Google Pay ya Paytm mein khulta hai
4. Jab paisa aata hai, app use invoice se match karke Tally mein receipt voucher khud post kar deta hai
5. Us invoice ka WhatsApp reminder cadence apne aap ruk jaata hai

Yahi do-tarfa flow raat 9 baje wali reconciliation session ko khatam karta hai, kyunki matching din bhar hoti hai na ki ek batch mein raat ko. Reconciliation kaise apne aap hoti hai, ye [Tally payment reconciliation mobile pe](/blog/tally-payment-reconciliation-on-mobile/) wali guide khol deti hai. Aur reminder ka asar kaise hota hai, ye [udhaar vasuli kaise kare](/blog/udhar-vasuli-kaise-kare-distributor/) wala playbook detail mein bataata hai.

UPI ka ek sneaky cost hota hai jo distributor aksar miss karte hain. MDR, yaani har transaction pe banking cut. Takkada par hum saaf likhte hain: 0% MDR on UPI collections, no transaction cap, no monthly fee. Iska matlab ₹50,000 ho ya ₹5,00,000, vasooli pe koi percentage nahi katta.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Tally mobile par kaise chalaye, free mein ho sakta hai?**

A: Sabse sasta tarika ek remote desktop app jaise AnyDesk hai, jo office screen ko phone pe mirror kar deta hai bina licence cost ke. Quick check ke liye theek hai, par patli network pe chalta nahi aur chhoti screen pe theek se kaam nahi hota. View aur vasooli dono ke liye companion app hi practical raasta hai.

**Q: Kya phone pe Tally chalane ke liye alag licence lagta hai?**

A: Nahi. Companion app aapki maujooda Tally installation se hi judta hai, koi extra Tally licence nahi lagta. Aap sirf companion tool ki fees dete hain agar wo paid hai.

**Q: Office PC band ho jaaye to phone pe Tally data dikhega?**

A: Companion app mein cached data dikhta rehta hai, isliye outstanding aur ledger phone pe khulta rahega. Aapke phone pe banaye invoice ya receipt queue mein ruk jaate hain aur jaise hi PC on hota hai, sync ho jaata hai aur Tally mein post ho jaate hain.

**Q: Phone se banaya invoice Tally mein double entry to nahi banata?**

A: Nahi. Read-and-write companion mein write ek hi baar post hota hai office Tally mein, last_update wali clash app sambhal leta hai. Counter pe banaya invoice office machine pe ek saaf voucher banta hai, do baar nahi.

**Q: UPI se vasooli karne pe kitna cut katta hai?**

A: Takkada par 0% MDR on UPI collections, no transaction cap, no monthly fee. Yaani transaction par koi percentage nahi katta, na koi monthly fees. WhatsApp reminder ki cadence kaise lagti hai, ye [WhatsApp payment reminder](/blog/whatsapp-payment-reminder-for-distributors/) wali guide mein hai.

**Q: Mera Tally data phone pe safe rehta hai?**

A: Companion app aapke Tally data ka ek working slice sync service ke through phone pe laata hai. Connect karne se pehle vendor se teen cheez poochein: data kis India region mein store hota hai, unki taraf se kaun padh sakta hai, aur band karne pe deletion policy kya hai.
