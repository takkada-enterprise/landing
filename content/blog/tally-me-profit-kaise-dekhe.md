Tally me profit kaise dekhe, iske teen raste hain. Item ka profit: Stock Summary kholo aur F7 dabao. Bill ka profit: Sales Register mein mahina chuno aur F7 dabao. Mahine ka profit: Profit & Loss mein Alt+N se monthly column lagao. Phone par Takkada mein har invoice line ka margin dikhta hai. Teeno report ek hi books se banti hain, par sawaal alag hain. Item wali report batati hai kaunsa maal kamaata hai, bill wali batati hai kaunsa invoice kamaaya, aur mahine wali batati hai business ne us mahine kya kamaaya. Neeche teeno ke steps hain, ek paint aur hardware distributor ka example hai jisme Wall Putty 20 kg teen customers ko bika aur ek ko ₹530 mein gaya jabki cost ₹540 thi, aur yeh bhi ki Stock Summary aur Sales Register ka profit kabhi kabhi alag kyun aata hai. Aakhir mein phone par item se customer aur customer se invoice tak profit kaise dekhte hain, woh bhi hai.

## Key Highlights

- Item ka profit Stock Summary + F7 (Show Profit) se, bill ka profit Sales Register + mahina + F7 se, aur mahine ka profit Profit & Loss + Alt+N (Auto Column) se
- Dono report ki cost item ki stock valuation method se aati hai, isliye method badalne par profit badal jaata hai
- Takkada mein invoice par Margin % bikri ke daam par hai, aur sirf woh log dekhte hain jinko aap profit dikhana chahte ho

## Tally me profit dekhne ke teen raste

| Sawaal | Tally report | Key |
|---|---|---|
| Kaunsa item kitna kamaata hai? | Stock Summary | F7 (Show Profit) |
| Kaunsa bill kitna kamaaya? | Sales Register, mahina kholo | F7 (Show Profit) |
| Mahine mein business ne kitna kamaaya? | Profit & Loss A/c | Alt+N (Auto Column), Monthly |
| Kaunsa customer kitna kamaata hai? | Ledger Movement Analysis | Alt+G se kholo |

## Tally me item wise profit kaise dekhe?

1. Gateway of Tally se Stock Summary kholo, ya Alt+G dabake Stock Summary type karo.
2. F2 se period set karo, jaise 1 September se 30 September.
3. F7 (Show Profit) dabao. Outwards ke neeche har item ka Consumption, Gross Profit aur Perc column aa jaata hai.
4. Kisi group par Enter dabao toh uske items khulte hain.

Perc kis base par hai, yeh ek item par check kar lo: Gross Profit ko bikri ki value se aur Consumption se bhaag karke dekho, jo Perc se mile wahi base hai. Bikri ke daam par aur cost par percent ka fark [gross profit kaise nikale](/blog/gross-profit-kaise-nikale/) wale Hindi post mein samjhaya hai.

## Tally me bill wise profit kaise nikale?

1. Alt+G dabao aur Sales Register kholo.
2. Jis mahine ke bill dekhne hain, us mahine par Enter dabao.
3. F7 (Show Profit) dabao. Har voucher ke saath value, GST ke bina gross revenue, cost aur gross profit aa jaata hai.
4. Kisi bill par Enter dabao toh woh invoice khul jaata hai.

Tally ki business guide ke hisaab se F12 mein Show Gross Profit Estimates ka option bhi hai. Customer ka profit dekhna ho toh Alt+G mein Movement type karo aur Ledger Movement Analysis mein party chuno. Wahan party ne kaunsa item kis rate par liya, woh dikhta hai, par ek baar mein ek hi party.

## Tally me mahine ka profit kaise dekhe?

Profit & Loss A/c kholo, F2 se saal ka period lagao, phir Alt+N (Auto Column) dabake Monthly chuno. Har mahine ka alag column aa jaata hai. Dhyan rahe: jis mahine mein season ke liye bahut maal kharida, woh mahina ghate mein dikh sakta hai agar us mahine ka closing stock nahi gina gaya. Iska poora example English mein [month wise profit and loss in Tally Prime](/blog/month-wise-profit-and-loss-tally-prime/) mein hai.

## Example: ek item, teen customer, ek ghate mein

September 2026 mein Wall Putty 20 kg ki cost ₹540 per bag thi. Teen customers ko yeh bika:

| Customer | Bag | Average rate | Bikri | Profit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ravi Traders | 120 | ₹640 | ₹76,800 | ₹12,000 |
| Mehta Sanitary | 80 | ₹610 | ₹48,800 | ₹5,600 |
| Lakshmi Stores | 60 | ₹530 | ₹31,800 | −₹600 |
| **Kul** | **260** | | **₹1,57,400** | **₹17,000** |

Item ka kul profit ₹17,000 hai, jo bikri ka 10.8% hai. Stock Summary mein F7 yahi ek line dikhaata hai: Wall Putty, ₹17,000. Usme yeh nahi dikhta ki Lakshmi Stores ko har bag ₹10 ghate mein gaya. Woh dekhne ke liye Sales Register mein ek ek bill kholna padta hai, ya Ledger Movement Analysis mein ek ek party. Teen customer ho toh chal jaata hai, tees ho toh nahi.

## Dono report ka profit alag kyun aata hai?

Stock Summary aur Sales Register dono cost item ki stock valuation method se lete hain, jo item master mein set hoti hai, jaise Average Cost. Tally ki business guide kehti hai ki Stock Summary mein Alt+N (Auto Column) se Stock Valuation Methods chun kar dekh sakte ho ki method badalne se margin kaise badalta hai. Agar kisi item par purchase ki entry stock item ke bina hui hai, ya kisi item ka rate zero hai, toh cost galat aati hai aur dono report ka profit alag ho jaata hai. Pehle in entries ko theek karo, phir profit par bharosa karo. Steps aur reasons ka [English mein poora tareeka](/blog/item-wise-profit-in-tally-prime/) alag post mein hai.

## Mobile par: item se customer, customer se invoice

Takkada mein Reports tab ke Sales darwaze mein Sales by Item hai. Kisi item par tap karo toh uske customers aur unka is period ka average rate dikhta hai, phir customer par tap karo toh uske invoice. Upar wala example phone par aise hi dikhta hai: Wall Putty, phir Ravi Traders ₹640, Mehta Sanitary ₹610, Lakshmi Stores ₹530. Sales by Item mein customer ke return (credit notes) figures se ghat jaate hain aur alag Returns column mein dikhte hain. Grouping ek tap mein badalti hai: item, stock group, customer ya mahina. Yeh sab [party-wise item-wise sales report for Tally](/party-wise-item-wise-sales-report-tally/) page par screen ke saath hai.

Invoice kholo toh har line par Cost, Margin aur Margin % dikhta hai, jab owner settings mein Show margin on invoice on karta hai. Invoice par Margin % bikri ke daam par hai. Cost us financial year mein item ki woh cost hai jo aapki Tally lagati hai; Takkada apni taraf se cost nahi banata. Kisi line ki cost nahi hai, jaise naye item ki, toh Add cost dabake cost per unit daal do. Woh sirf us invoice ke liye lagti hai aur Tally mein nahi jaati. Owner aur full-access team members item-wise sales aur profit dekhte hain; baaki kaun reports dekhega, woh aap tay karte ho. Screens [invoice-wise profit for Tally](/invoice-wise-profit-tally/) page par hain, aur baaki saari reports [Tally reports on mobile](/tally-reports-on-mobile/) page par.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Tally me item wise profit kaise dekhe?**

A: Stock Summary kholo, F2 se period lagao aur F7 (Show Profit) dabao. Har item ke saath Consumption, Gross Profit aur Perc aata hai. Example mein Wall Putty 20 kg ka September profit ₹17,000 tha.

**Q: Tally me bill wise profit kaise nikale?**

A: Sales Register kholo, mahine par Enter dabao aur F7 (Show Profit) dabao. Har bill ke saath cost aur gross profit aa jaata hai. Takkada mein saved sales invoice kholte hi har line ka margin dikhta hai, agar owner ne Show margin on invoice on kiya hai.

**Q: Tally me mahine ka profit kaise dekhe?**

A: Profit & Loss A/c mein Alt+N (Auto Column) dabao aur Monthly chuno. Har mahine ka alag column aata hai. Jis mahine ka closing stock nahi gina, woh mahina ghate mein dikh sakta hai.

**Q: Ghate mein bika item kaise pata kare?**

A: Tally mein Sales Register ke F7 profit column mein minus wali lines dhoondni padti hain, ek ek bill. Takkada mein Sales by Item ke Insights tab mein Selling below cost card hai: kaunse item purchase price se neeche bike aur kitna paisa gaya. Yahan cost woh purchase price hai jo Takkada aapki Tally se padhta hai. Example mein Lakshmi Stores ko ₹600 ghate mein maal gaya.

Takkada aapki Tally ki books se, last sync tak, har item, customer aur invoice ka profit phone par dikhata hai. [WhatsApp par message karo](https://wa.me/919435977777) aur apne hi invoice par margin dekho.
