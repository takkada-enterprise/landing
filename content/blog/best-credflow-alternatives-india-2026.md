## How To Evaluate a CredFlow Alternative

Before comparing names, fix the criteria. For an Indian distributor, the credflow alternatives that matter are judged on four things, not on dashboard polish.

1. UPI collection cost. The MDR on UPI receipts is the largest recurring line in the collections P&L. A half-percent difference on ₹10 crore is ₹5 lakh a year.
2. Phone-side invoicing. Can a salesman raise a GST invoice, e-invoice IRN, and e-way bill at the counter, or does the tool assume the invoice already exists.
3. Tally sync depth. How many clicks sit between "retailer pays" and "Tally is updated."
4. Reminder quality. Whether reminders are generic templates or change with due date and overdue status.

## Vyapar: Standalone Billing for Non-Tally Businesses

Vyapar is a standalone billing and accounting app for small businesses that do not run on Tally.

**Best for:** Small shops and businesses choosing a primary billing app rather than a Tally companion.

**Pros:** All-in-one billing, GST invoicing, inventory, works without Tally.

**Cons:** It replaces the books rather than sitting on top of Tally, so a distributor with years of Tally history would be migrating away from their system of record. See the [Vyapar alternative for distributors](/blog/vyapar-alternative-for-distributors/) for the trade-off in detail.
