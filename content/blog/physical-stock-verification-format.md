A physical stock verification format has two copies. The counter's copy carries the date, godown, stock group, item name, unit, counted quantity and the name of the person who counted. The checker's copy adds the book quantity, the difference, the cost rate, the value of the difference, the reason and the name of the person who checked. The counter's copy never shows the book quantity, because a counter who can see what Tally says tends to write it down, and the shortage disappears from the sheet while it stays on the shelf. Make one sheet for each stock group in each godown, list every item in that group, including the ones Tally shows at zero, and keep the unit the same as in Tally. Below you will find the columns and who fills each, a filled sample of five spare parts, the summary to give your CA, and a blind count sheet to download and open in Excel.

## Key Highlights

- The counter's copy has seven columns and no book quantity. The checker's copy adds six more, from book quantity to the checker's name.
- One sheet covers one stock group in one godown, with every item listed, zero-stock items included, so the list itself gives nothing away.
- In the filled sample, 5 items are counted, 3 differ and the net shortage is ₹3,840 at cost.

## The columns, and who fills each

| Column | Who fills it | Why it is there |
|---|---|---|
| Date | Counter | Ties the count to a cut-off in Tally |
| Godown | Counter | Stock is checked one location at a time |
| Stock group | Counter | One group per sheet keeps the count small |
| Item | Printed before the count | Item names as they are in Tally |
| Unit | Printed before the count | Box, piece or kg, as Tally holds the item |
| Counted qty | Counter | What is on the shelf, zero included |
| Counted by | Counter | One person answers for the sheet |
| Book qty | Checker | Book stock in Tally at the cut-off |
| Difference | Checker | Counted minus book |
| Cost rate | Checker | The rate to value the gap |
| Value | Checker | Difference × cost rate |
| Reason | Checker | What explains the gap, after checking entries |
| Checked by | Checker | The second person who reviewed it |

## The counter's copy and the checker's copy

The counter's copy is the blind sheet. It holds the first seven columns only, and the counter takes it to the shelf. The checker's copy holds all thirteen and stays with you or your accountant. Print the counter's copy from a list of item names and units, never from the stock summary, which carries the book figure. If the counter finds something on the shelf that is not on the list, they write it at the bottom of the sheet with the quantity.

## How to fill the sheet

1. Before the count, fill the date, godown and stock group, and print every item and unit in the group.
2. The counter writes the counted quantity for each item, zero where the shelf is empty, signs and hands the sheet back.
3. The checker copies in the book quantity at the cut-off from Tally and works out each difference.
4. Any item with a difference is recounted by a second person on a fresh counter's copy.
5. The checker fills the cost rate, value and reason for every gap still left, and signs.
6. Correct the entries the reasons point to, then adjust what remains, as in [stock shortage and excess entry in Tally](/blog/stock-shortage-excess-entry-in-tally/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the format of physical stock verification?**

A: Two copies of one sheet per stock group per godown. The counter's copy has date, godown, stock group, item, unit, counted quantity and counted by. The checker's copy adds book quantity, difference, cost rate, value, reason and checked by.

**Q: What should a physical stock verification report contain?**

A: For each sheet: the godown, the group, the number of items counted and differing, the shortage and excess value at cost, the net, and who counted and checked. In the sample, 5 items counted, 3 differing and a net shortage of ₹3,840.

**Q: Should the counter see the book stock on the count sheet?**

A: No. A counter who can see the book figure tends to copy it, and a real shortage gets written over. Print the counter's copy from item names and units only, and keep the book quantity on the checker's copy.
