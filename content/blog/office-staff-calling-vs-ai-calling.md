Office staff calling vs AI calling comes down to this: your staff are better at the few parties who need judgement and a relationship, and the AI is better at asking the long list of everyone else, the same way every time, and writing every answer down. In Takkada, both work from the same overdue list in Send reminders now, built from your Tally books. With the Recovery dashboard on, your accountant logs each call's outcome, promise and note on the party, and the AI, started from the Call with AI button, logs the same kind of follow-up on its own, tagged AI, with a recording. Both land in the same place, so the owner sees one history per party, whoever made the call. The useful question is which parties go to which caller. This comparison walks through what each does well, what each leaves on record, and a simple way to split one overdue list between your office and the AI.

## The same list, two callers

Picture an overdue list of 60 parties. Six of them owe ₹1,00,000 or more each. Ten have a running dispute about returns or rates. The other forty-odd owe between ₹5,000 and ₹40,000 and simply have not paid yet.

Your accountant can make perhaps fifteen good calls on a normal day, between entries, GST work and the phone ringing with orders. The AI calls every party you tick, within the calling hours you set.

## Side by side

| | Office staff calling | AI calling |
|---|---|---|
| Who it suits | Big balances, disputes, long relationships | The long list of routine overdue parties |
| How many in a day | As many as the day allows | Every party you tick, within calling hours |
| What is said | Depends on who is calling | Your business name, the amount due, the bills, days overdue |
| Language | Whoever speaks it in the office | One of twelve, set once |
| What is recorded | Whatever the person logs | Outcome, promise, note and a recording, every time |
| Missed promise | Someone has to remember | Automatic re-call after the days you set, if switched on |
| Judgement calls | Yes: discounts, extra time, a visit | No. It records the dispute and passes it to you |

## Where your staff win

A party who has bought from you for fifteen years and owes ₹3,20,000 should hear from someone who knows him. So should a party with a real complaint about a damaged consignment. The AI does not negotiate, waive interest or offer a discount. Your accountant or the owner can, and those calls are worth their time.

## Where the AI wins

The AI does not get tired on the fortieth call or skip the awkward one. It names your business and the amount due from Tally on every call, in the language you set, and the answer is logged against the party with the outcome from the same list your team uses: Will pay, No answer, Wrong number, Disputes amount, Refused or Other. A promise is saved with its amount and date. A party who says they already paid has the claimed date and reference in the note. A party who asks not to be called again is not called again.

## Splitting the list

A simple split works well. Tick the big balances and known disputes for your staff. Tick the rest for Call with AI. When the results come back, Disputes amount and Refused go to the owner, Wrong number goes to the salesman for a fresh mobile, and promises wait until their date.
