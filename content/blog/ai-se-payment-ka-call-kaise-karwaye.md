AI se payment ka call kaise karwaye, iska seedha jawab hai: Takkada mein Payment Reminders kholo, Send reminders now pe jao, jin party ka payment baaki hai unko tick karo, aur Call with AI dabao. Confirm sheet pe kharcha dikhta hai, Call now dabate hi AI agent har party ko aapki chuni hui bhasha mein call karta hai, aapke business ka naam aur Tally ke hisaab se baaki amount batata hai, aur poochta hai ki payment kab karenge. Party jo bhi bole, woh us party pe follow-up ban ke aata hai: Will pay ke saath amount aur date, No answer, Wrong number, Disputes amount ya Refused, saath mein note aur call ki recording. Agar party ne date di aur payment nahi kiya, toh AI kuch din baad khud dobara call kar sakta hai. Matlab office ka koi aadmi phone pe nahi baithta, phir bhi har baaki wale party se baat hoti hai aur har jawab likha jata hai. Neeche poora tarika diya hai.

## Problem jo har distributor jaanta hai

Subah WhatsApp reminder gaya, do blue tick aa gaye, payment nahi aaya. Ab call karna padega. Lekin accountant entry mein busy hai, salesman ko dukaan wale se rishta kharab nahi karna, aur seth ji soch rahe hain lunch ke baad karenge. Lunch ke baad dispatch aa jata hai.

Jo call hoti bhi hai, uska koi record nahi. Party ne bola "Shanivar tak kar denge", aur woh baat ek aadmi ke dimaag mein reh gayi.

## Pehle settings set karo

Payment Reminders mein **AI calling** wala row milega. Isme yeh sab set hota hai:

- **Call again when a promise is missed:** party ne promise tod diya toh AI dobara call karega. Default mein on hai.
- **Days after the promised date:** promise ki date ke kitne din baad dobara call ho. Default 1 din.
- **Start calling from** aur **Stop calling at:** default subah 10 se shaam 6 tak.
- **Calling days:** default Somvar se Shanivar.
- **Max AI calls per party per month:** ek party ko mahine mein kitni baar call ho. Default 4.
- **Language:** baarah bhashaon mein se ek. Hindi, English, Assamese, Bengali, Odia, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada aur Malayalam.
- **Tone:** Polite ya Firm.

Phir **Save Settings** dabao.

## Call kaise lagaye

Payment Reminders mein **Send reminders now** kholo. Wahan har woh party dikhegi jiska paisa baaki hai, kitna overdue hai, kitne bill pending hain, aur sabse purana bill kitne din ka hai. Yeh amount wahi hai jo Bills Receivable mein dikhta hai.

Naam se search karo, ya Tally ke ledger group se filter karo. Party tick karo aur neeche **Call with AI** dabao. Sheet pe likha aayega "Call 15 parties with AI", aur kharcha bhi wahin dikhega. **Call now** dabao.

Thodi der mein "Queued 14 calls" aayega. Jis party ko call nahi gaya, uska naam aur wajah dikhegi: ledger pe phone number nahi hai, party ne call na karne ko bola hai, call pehle se queue mein hai, kuch baaki nahi hai, ya mahine ki AI call limit poori ho gayi.

## Call ke baad kya milta hai

Har call us party pe follow-up ban ke aata hai, AI ka tag laga hua, taaki pata chale yeh call AI ne kiya. Recovery dashboard on hai toh party ki timeline pe dikhega, aur History mein promise ka amount aur date. Nahi hai toh party ke bills page pe **AI call results** mein milega. Har AI call ki recording sun sakte ho.
