## ECR file kya hota hai

ECR yaani Electronic Challan cum Return. Jis business mein PF lagta hai, use har mahine EPFO ko do cheezein deni hoti hain: kis member ki kitni salary par kitna PF kata (return), aur uska paisa (challan). ECR dono ko ek file mein le aata hai. Payment ke baad har member ka hissa uske PF khaate mein jaata hai.

Zyadatar establishments mein employee ki salary (basic aur DA) ka 12% PF katta hai, aur employer bhi 12% deta hai. Employer ke 12% mein se 8.33% pension (EPS) mein jaata hai, aur baaki PF khaate mein. Pension wali salary ki seema (wage ceiling) 17 September 2026 se ₹15,000 se badh kar ₹25,000 mahina ho gayi hai, yeh Labour Ministry ne 16 September 2026 ko bataya. Isliye ECR mein EPF wages aur EPS wages alag columns hain.

## ECR mein kaun si details chahiye

EPFO ke ECR format mein har member ki line mein yeh fields hote hain:

| Field | Matlab | Dhyan rakho |
|---|---|---|
| UAN | Member ka Universal Account Number | Aapke establishment se juda hona chahiye |
| Member name | Naam, jaisa EPFO record mein hai | Spelling record se milao |
| Gross wages | Us mahine ki poori kamayi | Khaali nahi chhod sakte |
| EPF wages | Jis salary par PF katta hai (basic aur DA) | Gross wages se zyada nahi |
| EPS wages | Pension wali salary | EPF wages se zyada nahi, 17 September 2026 se ₹25,000 tak |
| EDLI wages | Insurance (EDLI) wali salary | 17 September 2026 se ₹25,000 tak |
| EPF contribution | Employee ka 12% | Poore rupee mein |
| EPS contribution | Employer ka 8.33% pension hissa | Poore rupee mein |
| EPF EPS difference | Employer ka baaki hissa | 12% minus pension hissa |
| NCP days | Jin dino ki salary nahi mili | Poore din, aadha din nahi |
| Refund of advances | PF advance wapas jama ho toh | Aam taur par 0 |

## Ek member ka worked example

Sunil ki basic salary ₹15,000 hai. October mein woh 1 din bina chhutti ke absent raha, toh 31 din ke mahine mein uski salary ₹14,516.13 bani. ECR mein figures poore rupee mein jaayenge:

| Field | Hisaab | ECR mein |
|---|---|---|
| Gross wages | 14,516.13 | 14516 |
| EPF wages | 14,516.13 | 14516 |
| EPS wages | 25,000 se kam hai, toh wahi | 14516 |
| EDLI wages | wahi | 14516 |
| EPF contribution (12%) | 14,516 × 12% = 1,741.92 | 1742 |
| EPS contribution (8.33%) | 14,516 × 8.33% = 1,209.18 | 1209 |
| EPF EPS difference | 1,742 − 1,209 | 533 |
| NCP days | 1 din absent | 1 |

Agar kisi ki EPF wages ₹30,000 hon aur PF poori salary par kat raha ho, toh EPS aur EDLI wages ₹25,000 par ruk jaate hain. Pension hissa 25,000 × 8.33% = ₹2,083 hota hai, aur employer ka baaki hissa 3,600 − 2,083 = ₹1,517 PF khaate mein jaata hai. Ceiling ka yeh badlav naya hai, toh pehle mahine ki file apne CA se zaroor check karwa lo.

## EPFO portal par upload ke steps

EPFO ke process flow ke hisaab se kaam is order mein hota hai:

1. EPFO ke Unified Portal par employer login karo
2. Payment menu mein ECR upload kholo
3. Wage month aur salary dene ki tareekh chuno, aur apni ECR text file chuno
4. Portal file validate karta hai. Galti ho toh line number ke saath batata hai, theek karke dobara upload karo
5. Validation ke baad TRRN banta hai. Statement verify karo
6. Challan prepare karo, rakam check karke Finalize karo. Challan mein employer ke EDLI aur admin charges bhi judte hain
7. Online payment karo. EPFO ke hisaab se monthly contribution sirf online jama hota hai

Portal ki screen aur button ke naam badalte rehte hain, toh screen par jo likha hai wahi follow karo. Payment 15 tareekh ke baad hua toh interest aur damages lag sakte hain, toh salary confirm hote hi file bana lo.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: ECR file kya hota hai?**

A: ECR yaani Electronic Challan cum Return, har mahine EPFO portal par upload hone wali file. Isme har PF member ka UAN, wages, PF aur pension ka hissa aur NCP days hote hain. Upload ke baad isi se challan banta hai.

**Q: ECR file kab tak upload karni hoti hai?**

A: EPFO ke employer booklet ke hisaab se contribution har mahine ki 15 tareekh tak jama hona chahiye, pichhle mahine ki salary ka. October ki salary ka PF 15 November tak. Der hone par interest aur damages lag sakte hain.

**Q: ECR mein NCP days kya hote hain?**

A: NCP yaani Non Contributory Period, woh din jinki salary nahi mili, jaise bina chhutti ke absent. 31 din ke mahine mein 2 din absent hon toh NCP days 2 likhte hain. Aadha din ECR mein allowed nahi, poore din likho.

**Q: ECR file kis format mein hoti hai?**

A: ECR ek text file hoti hai jisme har member ki ek line aur fields tay order mein hote hain. Saare amount poore rupee mein hone chahiye.
