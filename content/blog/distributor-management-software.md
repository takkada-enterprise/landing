Distributor management software for Indian businesses is an integrated operational layer that connects field order booking, mobile invoicing, WhatsApp invoice delivery, UPI payment collection, and automated payment reconciliation with a distributor's existing Tally accounting system. Distribution businesses operate across market routes, warehouses, and accounting desks. Without dedicated software, market orders rely on physical paper slips, invoices are typed manually in office desktop Tally, payment reminders depend on manual memory, and accountants spend hours matching bank credits against open ledger bills. Takkada operates around existing Tally Prime or Tally ERP 9 books, allowing field salesmen to take orders on mobile devices, generate e-invoices and e-way bills from the market, dispatch invoice PDFs automatically on WhatsApp with zero-MDR UPI payment links, and auto-reconcile receipts directly into Tally. This provides business owners with real-time route visibility, salesman performance tracking, and automated cash collection without replacing their core Tally accounting software.

Running a distribution business involves much more than accounting.

Orders are taken in the market. Invoices are generated at the office. Customers need payment reminders. Salesmen need access to customer information. Payments arrive through UPI and bank transfers. Accountants then have to reconcile everything with Tally.

Takkada connects these workflows around the Tally system distributors already use.

## One system around your Tally

Takkada connects:

`Sales → Orders → Invoices → WhatsApp → Payments → Reconciliation → Tally`

The goal is not to replace Tally. It is to make the business around Tally easier to operate.

### Customer order management
Distributors can give retailers a self-order link where they can view the catalogue, select quantities and submit an order. The distributor reviews the order and approves it into Tally as a sales order.

### Mobile invoicing
Invoices, delivery challans and other vouchers can be created from the phone, allowing teams to operate away from the office.

### WhatsApp invoice delivery
Once an invoice is created, it is sent to the customer's WhatsApp along with a payment link. Learn how [Tally WhatsApp integration](/blog/tally-whatsapp-integration/) automates invoice dispatches.

### Customer collections
Customers can pay through zero-MDR UPI payment links attached to invoices and reminders, while the distributor receives money directly into their business bank account.

### Automated reconciliation
Once payment arrives, Takkada identifies the invoice it belongs to and posts the receipt entry back into Tally automatically.

### Salesman workflows
Distributors can use Takkada for field operations, including beat routes, customer visit check-ins, order booking, and collection tracking. Explore our dedicated [salesman app for Tally](/salesman-app-tally/).
