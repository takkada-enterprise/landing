## The four facts on one visit record

A visit record that survives being questioned carries four things, together, on one artefact.

**Who.** The record has to say who was holding the phone. Raju, not a shared handset. Five salesmen on this book.

**Where the device said.** Latitude and longitude with accuracy, captured as the photo is taken, not typed. Gupta Stores does not carry a pin the app measures against.

**When.** Device time, so Station Road reads as a sequence. 10:40 at Gupta Stores, 11:05 at the next kirana.

**A photograph of the place.** Taken at the counter, showing the shutter and often the retailer in the doorway. Gupta himself will recognise his premises when the owner forwards it.

These four travel together. Field evidence gets screenshotted into a WhatsApp group and into a call with the retailer. A photo carrying its own caption plate stays true after somebody saves it.

Tapping the visit photo shows that plate: salesman, an approximate street address prefixed "Approx." (looked up when viewed), coordinates with accuracy, and the timestamp. The stored fact is the coordinates. The address is derived later, because in a Fancy Bazaar lane the lookup will name the wrong gali often enough to matter.
