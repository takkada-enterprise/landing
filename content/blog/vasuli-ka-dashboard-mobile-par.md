## Vasuli ka dashboard mobile par kaise dekhe?

Phone uthhao, Collect kholo, Recovery kholo. Wahi vasuli dashboard hai. Char tabs milte hain. Parties aaj ki ageing party ke hisaab se, slabs edit ho sakti hain. Bills wahi paisa voucher-voucher. Habits har customer kitne din mein pay karta hai, median days, Reports+ ka hissa. Team pe Recovered, Outstanding, Speed matlab median days to collect, dealers worked, promises kept missed open, rank, company ka roll-up, aur ek line un overdue parties ki jinko kisi ne chase nahi kiya.

Ye [payment recovery dashboard](/blog/payment-recovery-dashboard-for-distributors/) wahi cheez hai, English hub pe detail hai. Yahan sawaal seedha hai: mobile par dikhe kaise. Tally desktop band hai, owner scooter pe hai, chemist ne kal ₹45,000 bola tha. Recovery us sentence ko brief pe laata hai. Ageing list usko row 40 pe chhod deti.

[Bakaya kaise vasool kare](/blog/bakaya-kaise-vasool-kare-distributor/) wala kaam cadence aur UPI se chalta hai. [Udhar vasuli](/blog/udhar-vasuli-kaise-kare-distributor/) wala kaam credit rule se. Vasuli dashboard un dono ke beech subah ka view hai: aaj kiska promise hai, kisko kisi ne chhua nahi. Tool pehle nahi, list pehle.

## Vasuli dashboard ke char tabs

**Parties.** Retailer ke naam pe bakaya, aaj ke hisaab se. 21-day terms wali book ko 30-day bucket mein force nahi karna, slabs change kar sakte ho. Ye wahi ageing hai jo owner pehle se samajhta hai, ab phone pe working list.

**Bills.** ₹1,20,000 koi mood nahi, teen invoice hain. Chemist ne ₹45,000 promise kiya to kisi named bill ke against. Jab tak receipt allocate na ho, woh bill yahin baitha rehta hai.

**Habits.** Do party dono 62 din pe ho sakti hain, kaam alag. Ek hamesha 60-70 pe full bill deti hai. Doosri pehle 25 din mein deti thi, is saal pehli baar 62 pe aa gayi. Days-to-pay aur median days yahi dikhate hain. Slow-and-honest ko slipping jaisa mat treat karo.

**Team.** Cards, sort chips. Har aadmi pe Recovered, Outstanding, Speed, dealers, promises, rank. Company roll-up. Aur woh line: overdue parties nobody has chased. Monday ki meeting yahin baithti hai.

Tally neighbour hai, dushman nahi. Recovery Tally ke parties aur bills padhti hai. Nayi kitaab nahi banati. Owner field aur follow-up log extra hain, chase ke liye, voucher ke liye nahi.

## Subah ka card: aaj kya due hai

Daily brief ek card hai jo owner kholta hai. Usme ho sakta hai: aaj due promises, aaj due follow-ups, proven missed promises, receipts awaiting confirmation, top overdue parties. Kuch na ho to All clear likha aata hai.

August ki kisi Tuesday ko Guwahati FMCG book aisa padh sakti hai. Ek promise aaj due: Barpeta Chemist, ₹45,000, kal call pe log hua. Do follow-up: Dibrugarh kirana Saturday ko no answer, Tezpur grocer rate pe disputed. Ek missed: Nalbari wale ne Friday bola, ledger abhi khali. Teen overdue, koi recovery owner nahi.

Wahi subah hai. 280 ledger scroll karke start mat karo. Jo line aaj hil sakti hai, usse start karo. Accountant ko bhi yehi chhah line dikhani hon to share sheet se text bhejo, WhatsApp ho ya email. Share tum karte ho. Card khud nahi nikalta.

Receipts awaiting confirmation wahi line hai jo galat chase rokati hai. Raat 11:40 pe UPI aa gaya, Tally mein 8:10 pe chadhega. Subah chemist ko "bhai last night bhej diya" sunne se pehle ye line padh lo. Paisa bank mein ho, kitaab peeche ho, to speech mat do.

Poora mechanic [daily collection brief](/blog/daily-collection-brief-for-distributor-owners/) pe English mein pada hai. Yahan itna kaafi hai: owner card kholta hai, haath se share karta hai.

## Follow-up log aur promise

Har party pe follow-up log. Channel: call, WhatsApp, in person, other. Outcome: paid, promised, no answer, disputed. Next-action date. Optional promised amount aur promised date. Entries append-only. Naya promise purane ko supersede karta hai. Friday wala "kal bhej dunga" Tuesday ko edit karke naya sentence nahi banta. Aaj ki line add hoti hai, aaj ka promise chalta hai.

Monday 7 PM owner call karta hai, chemist ₹45,000 kal bolta hai. Log: channel call, outcome promised, amount ₹45,000, date Tuesday. Subah 8:40 brief us promise ko due today dikhata hai. Receipt aa gaya to chase khatam. Nahi aaya to due hai, broken nahi, jab tak date nikal na jaaye.

Proven missed tab: promised date nikal gayi, Tally mein us party ka receipt nahi, sync lag allow karke. Midnight pe flag nahi. 11:40 PM ki UPI jo 8:10 AM pe Tally mein aaye, woh payment hai, toota wada nahi.

Log ageing ko conversation banata hai. DSO 58 se 64 company ka number hai. "Is hafte teen promise miss, saare Ramesh ki parties" Tuesday ka plan hai. Detail [follow-up log](/blog/customer-follow-up-log-for-collections/) aur [promise tracking](/blog/promise-to-pay-tracking-distributors/) pe hai. Mobile par itna yaad rakho: party kholo, line likho, next date daalo.

## Team board aur jis party ko kisi ne nahi chheda

Team pe har aadmi ka card. Recovered kitna aaya. Outstanding aaj kya baaki. Speed median days to collect. Do salesman ₹6 lakh recover karein to bhi Speed alag ho sakta hai, ek ne 20-din ka paisa nikala, doosre ne 90-din ka. Rank, dealers worked, promises kept missed open.

Sabse underrated line: overdue parties nobody has chased. ₹18 lakh overdue hamesha dikhega. Figure ye nahi batata kaunsi party pe is mahine na call hua, na WhatsApp, na visit, na owner. Woh line batati hai. Tuesday ko teen naam: Nagaon 94 din, Goalpara 71, Tinsukia 88.

Monday ki baat badal jaati hai. Pehle: collection thoda tight karo. Ab: ye teen chhui nahi gayi, Ramesh Nagaon aur Goalpara lo, Tinsukia main lunga, recovery owner app se assign karke nikalna. Agle Monday unhi cards pe dikhega Recovered hila ya nahi, promise rakha gaya ya nahi, unchased line chhoti hui ya nahi. Nagaon pe Saturday ka no-answer log ho gaya to woh line se utar jaata hai, chahe ₹2,40,000 abhi khula ho. Paisa alag conversation hai. Unchased band hona log aur owner se hota hai, Tuesday ki meeting ke baad field khali mat chhodna.

Salesman field mein apni parties dekhta hai. Owner Team pe dekhta hai kisne recover kiya, kaun slow hai, kisko poocha nahi. Dono same party master, Tally wala.
