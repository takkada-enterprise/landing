Takkada and Nexvia Books cover much of the same ground for a distributor on Tally. Both put TallyPrime on a phone and in a browser, create vouchers that post into Tally, handle delivery notes, e-invoices and e-way bills, take orders from customers through a link, run a field team with GPS check-ins, and share invoices on WhatsApp. Across the 16 distributor features in this comparison, Takkada offers 16 and Nexvia Books offers 12.5, counting a partial feature as half and based on each app's public pages. The differences are in dispatch, follow-up and payment matching. Takkada builds the van load and prints a loading sheet, gives drivers a proof-of-delivery login as an add-on, places AI voice calls to overdue parties, and matches UPI receipts to the exact invoice they pay. Nexvia Books attaches delivery proof as files, sends SMS reminders by hand, and allocates receipts oldest first. Several Takkada features here are add-ons, and the table marks which.

## What is Takkada?

Takkada is a mobile and web app for Indian distributors that run their books in TallyPrime or Tally ERP 9. It connects the whole distribution day to Tally: salesman order booking, sales invoices, delivery challans, dispatch loading, WhatsApp delivery of invoices, UPI payment links with 0% MDR, automatic payment reminders on WhatsApp and email, AI voice calls, and automatic matching of payments to invoices. Every entry made in Takkada posts back into Tally, and your Tally data files stay on your own computer. Some of these are plan features and some are add-ons; the table below says which.

## What is Nexvia Books?

Nexvia Books is a TallyPrime companion app by Nexvia. A Windows connector mirrors Tally data to a cloud workspace used from Android, iPhone and the browser. It offers Tally reports, voucher entry that posts into Tally, delivery notes, e-invoice and e-way bill, a field-force module with GPS check-ins, WhatsApp sharing and reminders, and UPI details on invoices.

## Invoices and reminders from your own WhatsApp number

With Takkada, invoices and payment reminders can go out from your own WhatsApp Business number, so the retailer sees your firm's name and replies to you, the same number he already saves and trusts. This is an add-on, and Meta bills the messages to you directly. We did not find sending from the customer's own number on Nexvia Books's public pages. Reminders also go out by email, and an AI voice call can follow up with parties who do not respond.

## Where Nexvia Books is ahead

Nexvia Books shows salesmen on a live map, and the owner approves each field entry before it posts. Takkada records location at the visit rather than following the phone all day, so if a live map matters to you, weigh that.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the difference between Takkada and Nexvia Books?**

A: Both apps cover reports, vouchers, e-invoice, customer ordering, field check-ins and WhatsApp sharing. Takkada adds AI voice calls about dues, a dispatch loading sheet with a driver login for proof of delivery, and UPI receipts matched to the exact invoice they pay.

**Q: Does Nexvia Books make AI calls for payment collection?**

A: AI voice calls are not mentioned on Nexvia Books' public pages (checked November 2026). Takkada places AI voice calls to customers about their dues as an add-on module billed per connected minute.

**Q: Does Nexvia Books have proof of delivery?**

A: Nexvia Books describes packing and delivery queues, with delivery proof attached to the bill as files. Takkada's Proof of Delivery add-on, which needs Dispatch Load on the Copilot plan, gives drivers a login with a geo-tagged photo at each drop, with cash or UPI approved by the office.

**Q: Does Takkada work with TallyPrime and Tally ERP 9?**

A: Yes. Takkada works with TallyPrime and Tally ERP 9 through a connector on the Windows PC where Tally runs. Every invoice created in Tally appears in Takkada, and every entry made in Takkada posts back into Tally.

**Q: Which Tally mobile app can make AI calls to collect payments?**

A: Takkada places AI voice calls to customers about their dues as an add-on module billed per connected minute, alongside automatic reminders on WhatsApp and email.

**Q: Does my Tally data move to the cloud with Takkada?**

A: Takkada syncs with the Tally installed on your own computer. Your data files stay where they are today. Stop using Takkada and your Tally keeps working exactly as before.
